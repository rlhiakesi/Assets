HARU CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION IAKESI Revised Edition

PURPOSE This file is the binding textual reference for Haru in Chapter
7. Use it together with Haru-CH7-character-sheet.png and
Haru-CH7-expression-sheet.png. If an incidental rendering conflicts with
this specification, this specification controls unless the script
explicitly establishes a temporary change.

IDENTITY Name: Haru (晴) Race/Nationality: Earth human; Japanese Sex:
Male Age during Chapter 7: 36 Height: Approximately 6 feet 0 inches (183
cm) Build: Lean, muscular, and strong from physical farm labor; not
bodybuilder-large Eyes: Dark brown Hair: Black Facial hair: Short beard
and mustache Role: Emi’s husband; Midori’s father; Aokawa farmer
Historical setting: Kamakura-era Japan Status at beginning of Chapter 7:
Living human Status at end of Chapter 7: Deceased on Earth; chooses Door
3 and becomes an Acolyte

STORY FUNCTION Haru is Midori’s father and Emi’s equal partner. He
represents responsibility, practical courage, and devotion to family and
village. When Aokawa faces starvation, Haru volunteers to travel to
Kamakura and petition for relief. Emi independently insists on
accompanying him. Haru tries to reason with Truk rather than provoke
him, but his practical criticism of Truk’s policy is treated as
defiance. Truk orders Haru and Emi executed. Haru’s death is a defining
event for Midori and Takeshi.

PERSONALITY Haru is hardworking, disciplined, patient, compassionate,
practical, principled, humble, and deeply devoted to his family. His
strength is quiet rather than theatrical. He avoids needless
confrontation, but when something is morally important he speaks plainly
even when doing so is dangerous. He does not seek glory, status,
revenge, or conflict.

FACE AND HEAD Apparent age: Mid-thirties Face: Strong, mature, weathered
but kind; defined jawline and working-man appearance Eyes: Dark brown;
never glowing unless a script explicitly establishes a temporary effect
Hair: Black, medium-long, normally tied back in a practical loose
bun/topknot Loose strands: Several loose strands naturally frame the
face Facial hair: Short beard and mustache; keep length and shape
consistent Complexion: Natural lightly weathered Japanese complexion
appropriate to outdoor farm work Default expression: Serious, attentive,
grounded, and quietly protective Ears: Normal human ears only; never
pointed or elven

BODY AND SILHOUETTE Canonical height is approximately 6 feet / 183 cm.
Binding silhouette: - tall adult male frame; - lean muscular build from
farm labor; - black tied-back hair with loose strands; - short beard and
mustache; - simple rural work clothing; - practical wrapped lower legs
and straw sandals; - grounded working-man posture. Haru must not look
like a samurai, soldier, fantasy warrior, aristocrat, or bodybuilder.

CLOTHING — EARTH / AOKAWA Primary top: Simple practical kimono or work
robe in muted off-white, linen-gray, weathered blue-gray, or similarly
restrained rural tones Lower garment: Practical work trousers suitable
for farming and travel Sash/obi: Dark functional cloth sash Legs:
Practical cloth wrappings where shown Footwear: Straw sandals / waraji
Cold-weather option: Simple practical wrap or cloak when required by
weather Condition: Well maintained but visibly used, mended, and
appropriate to a poor rural household Jewelry: None by default Armor:
None Rank insignia: None Personal sword: None

VILLAGE-OWNED TRAVEL WEAPON Haru does not personally own a warrior’s
sword. Before the journey, the Village Chief issues Haru a practical
weapon from Aokawa’s communal stores because the road to Kamakura is
dangerous. The weapon remains village property. At the Kamakura
government compound, Haru is ordered to surrender the weapon. He
hesitates because it belongs to Aokawa, then complies. The weapon is not
returned to Aokawa after Haru’s death. Its loss later angers the Village
Chief and is part of Chapter 8 continuity. Do not portray the issued
weapon as proof that Haru is a samurai or professional fighter.

EXPRESSIONS Canonical useful expressions include neutral, calm/gentle,
warm smile, concerned, worried, sad, determined, resolve, thoughtful,
protective, restrained anger, tired, pain/grief, loving toward Emi,
loving/protective toward Midori, profile left/right, and three-quarter
left/right. Avoid exaggerated rage, heroic battle poses, slapstick
distortion, villainous expressions, or youthful anime affectations.

POSTURE AND MOVEMENT Haru moves like a farmer accustomed to physical
labor. His hands and posture should suggest years of work. He carries
baskets, tools, supplies, and travel gear naturally. His gestures are
restrained and purposeful. During the petition he kneels and behaves
respectfully. When confronting Truk, his challenge remains verbal and
practical; he does not posture like a warrior.

RELATIONSHIPS Emi: Wife and equal partner. Haru volunteers to travel to
Kamakura; Emi independently chooses to accompany him. Midori: Daughter
and central emotional priority. Midori addresses him as Otou-san.
Takeshi: Trusted family friend and son of the Village Chief. Village
Chief: Aokawa’s local leader. Haru trusts his assurance that Midori will
be cared for and accepts the village-owned travel weapon from him. Truk:
Kamakura official who rejects Aokawa’s petition and orders Haru and Emi
executed. Terra: Earth’s Goddess. Once Haru understands Terra’s divine
status in the SRA, he naturally addresses her as Terra-sama.

CHAPTER 7 CONTINUITY Haru and Emi travel to Kamakura on foot over a
journey lasting weeks through difficult mountainous country. They do not
ride horses. At the Kamakura compound, Haru surrenders the village-owned
weapon. Haru’s fatal argument is practical: if Truk takes what little
remains, there will be nothing left to tax the following year. Truk
interprets this as defiance and orders Haru and Emi executed. Takeshi
draws his bow intending to intervene but is discovered and forced to
flee. Haru dies in Chapter 7.

SOUL RELOCATION AREA CONTINUITY At the moment of their deaths, Haru and
Emi arrive in the Soul Relocation Area (SRA), distinct from the WOA and
COG. Their arrival is an ordinary Earth-soul arrival. Terra receives
them as Earth’s Goddess performing normal SRA duty. Door 1 returns a
soul to Earth in a new infant body without prior-life memories. Door 2
sends a soul to another world with memories retained in a newly created
body corresponding to the Earth body at death. Door 3 keeps the current
spiritual form while the soul becomes an Acolyte. Emi chooses to remain
first. Haru independently agrees. Together they become Acolytes. Haru
and Emi are elsewhere when Midori later reaches the SRA in Chapter 8 and
do not reunite with her until much later.

LANGUAGE AND HONORIFICS Midori may address Haru as Otou-san. Once Haru
recognizes Terra as a Goddess, he addresses her as Terra-sama. Japanese
humans habitually use -sama when directly addressing a recognized
Goddess.

PROHIBITED DRIFT - No age younger than the established mid-thirties
appearance. - No height substantially different from approximately 6
feet except incidental perspective. - No fantasy-colored hair. - No
clean-shaven face unless explicitly required later. - No full heavy
beard. - No pointed ears. - No samurai armor, noble clothing, personal
samurai sword, paired swords, rank insignia, or clan crest. - No fantasy
weapon, staff, wand, magical aura, or glowing eyes. - Do not depict Haru
as knowing or practicing magic while alive on Earth. - No horse travel
to Kamakura. - Do not depict Haru as forcing Emi to accompany him. - Do
not depict Haru and Emi as specially selected for the SRA. - Do not
imply Acolyte status immediately allows contact with Midori. - Do not
use later Acolyte clothing for living Haru.

RENDERING PRIORITY When detail must be simplified, preserve in this
order: 1. Mature mid-thirties face with short beard and mustache 2.
Black tied-back hair with loose strands 3. Approximately 6-foot lean,
muscular farmer’s build 4. Simple muted rural work clothing 5. Dark
practical sash, leg wraps, and straw sandals 6. Weathered hands and
grounded working-man posture 7. Restrained, protective emotional acting

VISUAL STYLE IAKESI target style: approximately 70% photorealistic / 30%
manga. Haru should look like a real hardworking farmer, not a fantasy
hero. Clothing may accumulate dust, rain, mud, wrinkles, and wear during
the journey, but the core identity must remain consistent. Execution
scenes should preserve dignity and avoid graphic gore.
