TERRA — CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION

Identity Terra is a female second-tier Goddess of Earth.

She is substantially more senior than the Neophytes Zorna and Nyssara
and serves as their mentor. Terra is also Alpha’s trusted special
assistant and advisor. She may attend meetings of the Council of Gods
and stand beside Alpha, but she is not one of the eight seated Council
members.

Apparent Age 45.

Terra is actually far older than this. Forty-five is the visual age
target.

Height Approximately 5 feet 7 inches (170 cm).

Build Mature, curvaceous adult build.

Terra has a fuller and more physically substantial figure than Zorna or
Nyssara. Preserve the general proportions of her approved character
sheet. She should look mature, healthy, strong, and feminine without
exaggerating her proportions.

Face An attractive, clearly mature woman of approximately 45 apparent
years.

Terra should not look like a young adult who has merely been labeled 45.
Use mature adult facial proportions, defined cheekbones and jaw, mature
eyes, and subtle age-appropriate character in the face.

Do not add heavy wrinkles or make her look elderly.

Her default expression is warm, intelligent, composed, and observant.

Eyes Moss green / warm olive green.

The eye color should remain earthy and warm rather than bright emerald
or neon green.

Skin Warm fair skin.

Ears Normal human-shaped ears.

Neither pointed nor elongated. Never render Terra with elf-like or
fae-like ears.

Hair Auburn/copper.

Terra has very thick, voluminous “big hair” composed of large loose
waves and curls.

The hair is slightly shorter than shoulder length. It should create a
broad, rounded, high-volume silhouette without becoming long flowing
hair.

Terra does not have Zorna’s drill/corkscrew curls.

Terra’s hair does not use Nyssara’s straight-hair silhouette.

Hair Part Terra has a strong side part rather than a center part.

A large swept wave crosses the forehead from the side part, creating an
asymmetrical front silhouette.

Do not convert Terra’s hairstyle into a center part resembling Zorna.

Hair Ornament Terra wears one small living-leaf hair ornament.

The ornament consists of a small, simple cluster of fresh green leaves
positioned to complement the side-swept hairstyle.

It is not a wreath, crown, tiara, circlet, vine network, or elaborate
floral arrangement.

The leaf ornament should remain simple enough to reproduce consistently.

The leaves are always fresh and vibrant.

Jewelry No jewelry in casual attire.

Do not add earrings, necklaces, bracelets, forehead jewels, tiaras,
crowns, or decorative gemstone accessories.

Terra’s authority should come from her appearance, rank, clothing, and
demeanor rather than jewelry.

Casual Outfit Terra’s casual outfit is her default attire.

It is substantially more refined and elaborate than the casual clothing
worn by Zorna and Nyssara, reflecting Terra’s higher rank as a
second-tier Goddess.

The dominant color is forest green, similar to the green used in the
corset area of Terra’s original design.

Secondary colors may include sage green, olive, cream, and restrained
gold.

The outfit is an elegant mature dress with a fitted upper body and
flowing lower portion.

A moderately plunging V-neckline reveals a modest amount of cleavage.
The neckline should communicate mature confidence without becoming
excessively revealing.

The dress may include: - Flowing or layered sleeves. - Organic
asymmetrical draping. - A shaped waist. - Restrained gold piping or
embroidery. - Sage, olive, or cream secondary panels. - Curved
construction lines inspired by natural growth.

The outfit should be visibly more sophisticated than the Neophytes’
everyday clothing but must remain reproducible across complex scenes.

Avoid excessive tiny ornaments, chains, dangling pieces, dense filigree,
or unnecessary costume complexity.

Casual Shape Language Terra’s visual shape language is organic, curved,
and asymmetrical.

Garment seams, drapes, and construction may suggest branching growth,
leaves, flowing water, or natural forms without literally covering the
costume in nature symbols.

Do not stamp generic leaf icons over the outfit merely to communicate
“Earth.”

Formal Outfit Terra’s formal attire preserves the overall concept of her
original ceremonial design.

The formal gown uses white/cream, forest green, and gold.

It is elaborate, layered, elegant, and clearly appropriate for a senior
Goddess.

The formal outfit may be dramatically more complex than her casual
clothing, with flowing fabric and ceremonial construction.

Remove the original tiara, forehead jewel, necklace, earrings, and
excessive dangling ornamentation.

The costume itself should provide the grandeur.

Terra retains her simple living-leaf hair ornament with formal attire.

Typical formal-use cases include: - Formal appearances before the
Council of Gods when ceremony warrants it. - Work in the Soul Relocation
Area when presentation is intended to reassure or impress newly arrived
souls. - Formal appearances before mortals.

Default Outfit Rule Use Terra’s casual green outfit unless the script
explicitly specifies formal attire.

Magical Outfit Change Terra changes between casual and formal clothing
magically.

She rotates one hand in a circle.

A very brief magical-girl-like transformation changes her clothing
essentially instantaneously.

The transformation changes clothing only. It does not change her body,
face, hair, apparent age, or other physical characteristics.

Her living-leaf hair ornament remains present.

Posture Relaxed, grounded, and confident.

Terra is comfortable occupying space. Her stance may include a subtle
shift of weight onto one hip, relaxed shoulders, and naturally
positioned hands.

She does not need Zorna’s overtly commanding posture to establish
authority.

Terra should look like someone accustomed to being listened to.

Movement Calm, economical, and unhurried.

Terra rarely makes unnecessary gestures.

When she does act decisively, the contrast with her normal patience
should be noticeable.

Personality Expressed Visually Wise. Patient. Compassionate. Observant.
Nurturing. Protective. Balanced. Pragmatic. Experienced. Diplomatic.
Quietly authoritative.

Terra tends to see the larger problem rather than choosing between
obvious alternatives.

She generally guides rather than commands.

Her compassion does not make her passive. When life or fundamental
ethical principles are threatened, Terra can become extremely firm.

Relationship with Alpha Terra is Alpha’s trusted special assistant and
advisor.

Their familiarity should be visible in their body language. Terra does
not behave around Alpha with the same formality expected of ordinary
subordinates.

Alpha values Terra’s judgment and institutional memory.

Terra may stand beside Alpha during Council meetings and speak freely,
though she is not one of the eight seated Council members.

Relationship with Zorna Terra is Zorna’s mentor.

She recognizes Zorna’s leadership ability, ambition, confidence, and
willingness to act, while also recognizing Zorna’s tendency to impose
outcomes when she believes she knows what is best.

Terra teaches Zorna to guide circumstances rather than seize outcomes.

Relationship with Nyssara Terra is Nyssara’s mentor.

She recognizes Nyssara’s intelligence, caution, empathy, observational
ability, and tendency to discover solutions others overlook.

Terra encourages Nyssara to trust her judgment despite being less
experienced than Zorna.

Visual Differentiation from Zorna Zorna: - Younger apparent age. -
Taller/slender-athletic build. - Platinum-blonde hair. - Extremely long
drill/corkscrew curls. - Center/open forehead treatment. - Ice-blue
eyes. - Predominantly white casual outfit. - More commanding, vertical,
expansive shape language.

Terra: - Apparent age approximately 45. - Mature, fuller curvaceous
build. - Auburn/copper hair. - Slightly shorter than shoulder-length
“big hair.” - Large loose waves/curls. - Strong side part and
asymmetrical swept front. - Moss/olive-green eyes. - Forest-green casual
outfit. - Organic, curved, asymmetrical shape language.

Visual Differentiation from Nyssara Nyssara: - Apparent age
approximately 30. - Petite/slender adult build. - Straight black
shoulder-blade-length hair. - Bangs and chin-length front sections. -
Violet eyes. - Distinctly elven ears. - Lavender tunic and trousers. -
Contained, observational presence.

Terra: - Apparent age approximately 45. - Taller and fuller mature
build. - Shorter, voluminous auburn/copper hair. - Strong side part. -
Moss-green eyes. - Human ears. - Forest-green dress. - Warm, grounded
mentor presence.

Forbidden Visual Traits Do not render Terra with: - A youthful apparent
age comparable to Zorna or Nyssara. - An adolescent face or body. -
Elf-like, fae-like, or pointed ears. - Extremely long hair. -
Platinum-blonde or black hair. - Drill/corkscrew curls resembling
Zorna. - Straight hair resembling Nyssara. - A center part resembling
Zorna. - A tiara, crown, circlet, or wreath. - A forehead jewel. -
Earrings or necklaces in casual attire. - An elaborate vine network
woven through her hair. - Excessive jewelry in formal attire. - A casual
outfit dominated by white. - A casual outfit resembling a shrine maiden,
temple robe, school uniform, or Neophyte uniform. - Excessively
revealing casual clothing. - Excessive gold filigree or decorative
clutter. - Generic multi-point star/compass motifs or other recurring
AI-generated mystical ornament.

Production Reference Principle Terra should remain identifiable from
silhouette alone.

Her key silhouette anchors are her mature curvaceous build and her
slightly shorter-than-shoulder-length mass of large, voluminous auburn
waves with a strong asymmetrical side part.

From a head-and-shoulders view, the combination of mature facial
proportions, auburn big hair, strong side sweep, moss-green eyes, human
ears, and simple living-leaf ornament should make her immediately
recognizable.

Canonical character-sheet artwork and this textual specification should
be used together when generating or validating Terra’s appearance.
