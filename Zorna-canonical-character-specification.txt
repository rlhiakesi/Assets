ZORNA — CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION

Identity Zorna is a female Neophyte Goddess of the Zorans. She has been
a Neophyte longer than Nyssara and is currently the more experienced of
the two.

Apparent Age Young adult.

Build Tall, graceful, athletic/slender. Feminine figure with a moderate
bust. Her breast size must not be exaggerated or unusually large.

Face Refined oval face with a relatively narrow jaw. Mature and
self-assured rather than cute or childlike. Clear ice-blue eyes.

Skin Fair.

Ears Normal human-shaped ears. Neither pointed nor elongated. Never
render Zorna with elf-like, fae-like, or otherwise pointed ears.

Hair Platinum blonde. The platinum-blonde color is immutable and
plot-critical.

Her hair is extremely long, extending approximately to the thighs or
upper calves. Its defining feature is an abundance of large, tight
corkscrew/drill curls. The curls create a distinctive, high-volume
silhouette from the front, side, and back.

Some hair may be pulled back from the face while the majority falls
freely. The hairstyle must remain recognizably Zorna’s and must not
degrade into ordinary straight, loosely wavy, or generic long blonde
hair.

Jewelry None.

Zorna does not normally wear earrings, necklaces, tiaras, crowns,
bracelets, decorative gems, or hair jewelry.

Casual Clothing Zorna’s casual outfit is her default attire and is what
she wears most of the time.

It is predominantly white: a long, modest, relatively simple dress
intended for everyday activity. It has long sleeves, a high or otherwise
modest neckline, and a clean vertical silhouette.

Decoration and trim should be minimal. Small silver/gray and, where
useful, deep-blue accents are acceptable.

The casual costume must be easy for an image generator to reproduce
consistently, especially in complex scenes containing multiple
characters and environmental details.

Formal Clothing Zorna’s formal attire is an elaborate ceremonial version
of her visual identity.

White remains dominant, with deep-blue and gold accents or layers. The
formal costume may use flowing layers, a dramatic regal silhouette, and
substantially more elaborate construction than her casual clothing.

Formal attire is intentionally spectacular and should command attention.

Typical formal-use cases include: - Formal appearances before the
Council of Gods when ceremony warrants it. - Work in the Soul Relocation
Area when presentation is intended to reassure or impress newly arrived
souls. - Formal appearances before mortals on Zora.

Default Outfit Rule Use Zorna’s casual outfit unless the script
explicitly specifies formal attire.

Magical Outfit Change Zorna changes between casual and formal attire
magically.

She rotates one hand in a circle. A very brief magical-girl-like
transformation changes her clothing essentially instantaneously.

The transformation changes clothing only. It does not change her body,
face, hair, apparent age, or other physical characteristics.

Posture Upright, composed, and confident.

Zorna naturally occupies space as though she expects people to listen to
her. Avoid exaggerated heroic posing during ordinary conversation.

Movement Deliberate and economical. Zorna generally does not fidget.

Personality Expressed Visually Confident, ambitious, charismatic,
decisive, and commanding.

She expects competence and results. Her flaw is not deceitfulness or
habitual manipulation. Rather, Zorna tends to believe she knows what
should be done and is willing to act, intervene, and impose that
outcome.

Relationship Behavior with Nyssara Zorna is currently the more
experienced Neophyte and behaves subtly like an older sister toward
Nyssara.

She tends to take the lead, is more comfortable in unfamiliar
situations, and may be amused by Nyssara’s relative inexperience rather
than threatened by it.

This relationship is important because it will later reverse when Zorna
and Nyssara are reincarnated on Zora/Nyas and Nyssara becomes the older
sister.

Forbidden Visual Traits Do not render Zorna with: - Pointed, elongated,
elf-like, or fae-like ears. - Exaggerated or oversized breasts. - A
tiara or crown as part of her normal design. - Jewelry in her casual
attire. - Generic fantasy-goddess ornament overload. - Random gold
filigree or unnecessary decorative complexity in her casual outfit. -
The generic multi-point star/compass motif or similar recurring
AI-generated mystical ornament. - A hair color other than platinum
blonde. - Straight, merely wavy, or generic flowing hair replacing her
characteristic drill/corkscrew curls.

Production Reference Principle Zorna should remain identifiable from
silhouette alone, particularly through the distinctive mass and geometry
of her long drill curls.

She should also remain recognizable from a head-and-shoulders view
without relying on her costume.

Canonical character-sheet artwork and this textual specification should
be used together when generating or validating Zorna’s appearance.
