NYSSARA — CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION

Identity Nyssara is a female Neophyte Goddess of the Nyssarians. She has
been a Neophyte for less time than Zorna and, in Chapter 1, looks up to
Zorna somewhat like an older sister.

Apparent Age 30. This is a visual age target only; Nyssara is actually
far older.

Height Approximately 5 feet 4 inches (163 cm).

Build Petite adult build. Slender with natural adult curves and a modest
C-cup chest. She must look clearly adult and must not have adolescent
body proportions.

Face Adult feminine facial proportions appropriate to an apparent age of
approximately 30. Nyssara is intended to be the most naturally pretty of
the principal goddesses: an innocent beauty rather than a sophisticated
or glamorous beauty. Her face is soft, gently oval to subtly heart-shaped,
and somewhat more compact than Zorna’s, with soft cheeks and a small,
refined chin. She must remain clearly mature rather than childlike. Avoid
generic cute-anime-girl facial construction, oversized childlike eyes, or
adolescent proportions.

Eyes Violet. Her violet eyes are a major identifying feature and must
remain consistent. They are relatively large, clear, and expressive, with
a refined almond shape, detailed irises, delicate lashes, and soft outer
corners. They should contribute strongly to her innocent beauty without
making her look adolescent.

Skin Fair.

Ears Distinctly elven ears. Moderately long and elegantly tapered,
somewhat shorter than the ears in Nyssara’s original character design
but still unmistakably elven. Do not shorten them into subtle Vulcan- or
Romulan-like points. The ears should normally remain clearly visible
from front, 3/4, and side views.

Hair Black, relatively straight, and smooth. The main rear hair extends
approximately to the shoulder blades.

Nyssara has distinct bangs across the forehead. The bangs are a required
identifying feature and must not disappear.

Two front hair sections fall in front of the ears and terminate
approximately at chin level. Hair behind the ears continues to the
shoulder blades. The hairstyle keeps the elven ears exposed.

No braids, hair ornaments, ribbons, tiara, or crown.

Jewelry Nyssara wears no jewelry as part of her normal physical design.
The sole exception is the choker that is specifically part of her casual
outfit.

Casual Outfit Nyssara’s casual outfit is her default attire. It is a
simple, feminine blouse-and-skirt ensemble intended to complement rather
than compete with her natural beauty. It must not read as a robe,
shrine-maiden garment, temple garment, school uniform, or elaborate
fantasy costume.

The blouse is light lavender, sleeveless, fitted but not tight, and
nearly collarless. It has a modest V-neck that leaves the choker fully
visible and may reveal only the slight natural cleavage appropriate to
Nyssara’s modest C-cup build. The blouse is cleanly tucked into the
skirt. It has no sash, bows, ornamental harnesses, elaborate trim, or
complicated cutouts.

The skirt is darker lavender-purple, fitted cleanly through the waist and
hips with a restrained straight or subtly tapered silhouette. Its hem
falls just above the knees. A small restrained side slit is acceptable
for movement, but it must not become a thigh-high slit. Dark-plum
low-to-medium heels complete the casual outfit.

The casual outfit should feel understated, adult, graceful, and easy to
reproduce consistently. It should deliberately undersell Nyssara: Zorna
may appear more glamorous and sophisticated, while Nyssara should appear
more naturally pretty and innocent.

The lavender colors must remain bright enough that folds and contours
remain clearly visible under moderate shadows and after CMYK conversion.
Never allow the skirt to collapse into near-black or shadow-heavy dark
violet.

Casual Choker The choker is part of Nyssara’s casual costume, not her
anatomy.

It consists of one narrow dark-purple band and one smooth oval
opaque-white stone centered at the throat. No chain, pendant, filigree,
additional gemstones, or ornamentation.

Whenever Nyssara is shown in casual attire and her neck is visible, the
choker must be present, including full-body views, head-and-shoulders
shots, expressions, and hair/face close-ups.

Formal Outfit Nyssara’s formal attire is an elaborate ceremonial gown.
Deep violet is dominant. Lighter purple may be used for secondary layers
or accents. Gold trim is permitted but restrained compared with the
original costume.

Limited black or black-plum underlayers are acceptable, but the costume
should remain visibly purple and preserve detail in shadows.

The formal gown may be highly stylish and dramatic. Nyssara wears no
jewelry with it. The casual choker disappears during transformation into
formal attire.

Typical formal-use cases include formal appearances before the Council
of Gods, work in the Soul Relocation Area when formal presentation is
appropriate, and formal appearances before mortals on Nyas/Zora.

Default Outfit Rule Use Nyssara’s casual light-lavender blouse and
dark-lavender above-knee skirt unless the script explicitly specifies
formal attire.

Magical Outfit Change Nyssara rotates one hand in a circle. A very brief
magical-girl-like transformation changes her clothing essentially
instantaneously.

The transformation changes clothing only. It does not change her body,
face, hair, ears, apparent age, or other physical characteristics. The
casual choker disappears with formal attire and returns with casual
attire.

Posture and Movement Relaxed but attentive. Nyssara keeps her physical
presence more contained than Zorna. She may loosely clasp her hands or
hold one wrist with the opposite hand while listening.

She should not look timid, weak, submissive, or adolescent. Her body
language communicates that she watches and thinks before speaking.

Movement is graceful, precise, and economical.

Personality Expressed Visually Thoughtful, observant, intelligent,
cautious, collaborative, insightful, disciplined, quietly ambitious,
empathetic, strategic, gentle, and approachable.

Nyssara’s strength is not brute force or overt command. She listens,
analyzes, and often recognizes solutions others overlook. Her default
facial affect should have softness and openness: a neutral composed look
or very subtle natural smile rather than a smirk or overtly glamorous
confidence. Her beauty should read as innocent and largely unselfconscious,
not seductive, coquettish, or deliberately posed.

Relationship with Zorna Zorna is currently the more experienced
Neophyte. Nyssara looks up to her somewhat like a younger sister looks
up to an older sister.

Nyssara may watch Zorna for cues in unfamiliar situations, but she is
fully capable of disagreeing with her. She respects Zorna without being
subordinate to her.

This relationship later reverses when both are reincarnated on Zora/Nyas
and Nyssara becomes the older sister.

Visual Differentiation from Zorna Zorna is taller, platinum blonde, has
extremely long drill/corkscrew curls, human ears, ice-blue eyes, a
predominantly white casual dress, and an expansive commanding presence.

Nyssara is shorter and more petite, has straight black
shoulder-blade-length rear hair, distinct bangs, chin-length front
sections, clearly visible elven ears, violet eyes, a light-lavender
sleeveless blouse, dark-lavender above-knee skirt, casual choker, and a
more contained observational presence. Zorna is the sophisticated,
glamorous beauty; Nyssara is the innocent, natural beauty.

Visual Differentiation from Midori Nyssara must remain visually distinct
from Midori at both age 14 and adulthood.

Nyssara has an apparent age of approximately 30, clearly mature adult
proportions, violet eyes, distinctly elven ears, shoulder-blade-length
straight black rear hair, bangs, chin-length front sections, no red
ribbon, a light-lavender sleeveless blouse, dark-lavender above-knee
skirt, and a casual choker with a white oval stone.

Midori has human ears, dark-brown eyes, a petite build throughout life,
very long black hair, and her characteristic red hair ribbon.

Nyssara must never simply read as an older version of Midori.

Forbidden Visual Traits Do not render Nyssara with human-shaped ears;
tiny Vulcan/Romulan-style ear points; missing visible elven ears;
missing bangs; hair ornaments, ribbons, tiaras, or crowns; braided hair;
extremely long hair comparable to Zorna or Midori; adolescent
proportions; an exaggerated chest; shrine-maiden clothing; temple robes;
school-uniform-like casual clothing; a sash or bow on the casual outfit;
a floor-length casual dress resembling Zorna’s; a casual skirt much
shorter than just above the knee; a plunging or overtly sexual casual
neckline; a casual outfit so pale that it resembles Midori or Zorna; a
casual outfit so dark that shadow detail disappears; excessive gold
ornamentation; the casual choker with formal attire; a missing
choker in visible-neck casual views; or the generic multi-point
star/compass motif.

Beauty Direction Nyssara is the “innocent beauty” of the principal
goddesses. This is a core visual-design goal. She should be exceptionally
pretty without relying on glamour, elaborate styling, conspicuous makeup,
jewelry, or revealing clothing. Her appeal comes primarily from her face,
violet eyes, silky black hair, gentle expressions, and graceful presence.

Zorna remains the sophisticate: taller, more dramatic, polished, and
commanding. Nyssara should not imitate Zorna’s glamour. When the two are
shown together, Zorna may be the more immediately spectacular figure,
while Nyssara should read as the more naturally pretty and approachable
one.

Production Reference Principle Nyssara should remain identifiable from
silhouette alone.

From the side and back, her straight shoulder-blade-length hair,
chin-length front sections, petite adult build, and unmistakable elven
ears should distinguish her.

From a head-and-shoulders view, the combination of bangs, black hair,
violet eyes, exposed elven ears, mature adult face, and casual choker
should make her immediately recognizable.

Canonical character-sheet artwork and this textual specification should
be used together when generating or validating Nyssara’s appearance.
