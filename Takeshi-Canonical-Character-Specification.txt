TAKESHI CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION IAKESI Revised Edition

PURPOSE This file is the binding textual reference for Takeshi in
Chapters 7 and 8. Use it together with
Takeshi-character-reference-sheet.png and Takeshi-expression-sheet.png.
If an incidental rendering conflicts with this specification, this
specification controls unless the script explicitly establishes a
temporary change.

IDENTITY Name: Takeshi (たけし) Race/Nationality: Earth human; Japanese
Sex: Male Age during Chapter 7: 17 Height: 5 feet 7 inches (170 cm)
Build: Lean and athletic Birthplace: Aokawa Village Role: Village
tracker / hunter; son of the Village Chief Status during Chapter 7:
Living human

STORY FUNCTION Takeshi is the Village Chief’s son and a highly capable
young tracker. His father secretly assigns him to follow Haru and Emi to
Kamakura so Aokawa will know what happens to them. Takeshi witnesses
Truk reject their petition and order their execution. He ultimately
draws his bow intending to intervene, exposing himself and forcing him
to flee. He returns to Aokawa alive but withholds important details from
Midori. This begins a guilt and shame arc that continues in Chapter 8,
when Takeshi kills Truk and leaves Aokawa in shame. He later becomes the
legendary Gray Samurai, a Japanese Robin-Hood-like folk hero known by
the time Midori returns to Japan in 2012. Chapter 7 must not explicitly
name or foreshadow the Gray Samurai identity.

PERSONALITY Takeshi is quiet, observant, disciplined, fiercely loyal,
determined, capable, and serious beyond his years. He speaks little but
acts with purpose. He cares deeply for his family and village and
especially respects Haru and Emi. He is not hot-headed, but he can be
fierce when necessary. His later guilt comes partly from having tried
and failed to save Haru and Emi and partly from not telling Midori
everything afterward.

FACE AND HEAD Apparent age: Seventeen; clearly a late adolescent/young
man, not a mature adult Face: Young, angular, expressive, with strong
brows Eyes: Dark brown; focused and observant Hair: Black, long enough
for a messy practical topknot with loose strands Facial hair: None Ears:
Normal human ears only Default expression: Watchful, restrained, serious

BODY AND SILHOUETTE Height: 5’7” / 170 cm Build: Lean and athletic from
mountain travel, hunting, climbing, and village work Binding
silhouette: - youthful lean frame; - messy black topknot with loose
strands; - large dark scarf/neck wrap; - dark layered indigo/blue rural
travel clothing with subtle restrained pattern; - yumi and quiver; -
wrapped lower legs and waraji sandals; - traditional utility gear
carried at the waist. The silhouette may have a ninja-like visual
flavor, but Takeshi is not canonically a ninja. He is a skilled village
tracker and hunter.

CLOTHING Base clothing: Dark indigo/blue layered rural travel garments
appropriate to a Kamakura-era mountain village Pattern: Subtle
restrained traditional-looking pattern; never modern camouflage Scarf:
Large dark gray/charcoal neck wrap Lower garments: Loose practical dark
trousers/hakama-like workwear Legs: Cloth wraps Footwear: Waraji sandals
Condition: Worn, practical, traditional, and suitable for mountain
travel No armor No noble clothing No professional military uniform No
modern tactical fabrics or closures

MISSION EQUIPMENT Primary weapon: Yumi / long bow Ammunition: Quiver
with arrows Secondary tool: Small practical utility knife Other
equipment: Rope, water gourd, and a limited number of functional belt
pouches All equipment must look traditional and plausible for a
pre-modern setting: wood, cloth, leather, rope, straw, and metal where
appropriate. The gear may be visually dense enough to support the
tracker silhouette, but must never become modern tactical equipment.
Takeshi is skilled because he grew up hunting, tracking, and traveling
in the mountains, not because Aokawa maintains a professional scout or
ninja corps.

EXPRESSIONS Canonical expressions include: - neutral; - focused; -
serious; - determined; - skeptical; - concerned; - alert; - thinking; -
surprised; - annoyed; - slight smile; - warm smile; - anger; -
speaking; - shouting; - hurt; - tired; - looking down / thoughtful; -
eyes closed / calm; - profile. Expressions should remain recognizably
youthful. Avoid making him look like a hardened middle-aged warrior.

POSTURE AND MOVEMENT Takeshi moves quietly and efficiently through
mountain terrain. He can crouch, climb, track, observe from concealment,
move through snow and forest, carry a bow, and travel long distances.
His competence comes from rural mountain life. When he draws his bow at
the Execution Courtyard, it is a desperate moral decision to intervene,
not a professional assassination pose.

RELATIONSHIPS Village Chief: Takeshi’s father. Takeshi is loyal to him
and follows his covert instruction to shadow Haru and Emi. Haru and Emi:
Trusted family friends whom Takeshi deeply respects. Their deaths become
a source of lasting guilt. Midori: Younger family friend. Takeshi
returns with news of her parents’ deaths but does not tell her the
complete story. Midori notices his reluctance and later holds his lack
of candor against him. Truk: Kamakura official responsible for Haru and
Emi’s deaths. Takeshi later kills Truk in Chapter 8.

CHAPTER 7 CONTINUITY The Village Chief secretly orders Takeshi to follow
Haru and Emi to Kamakura and report what happens. Takeshi travels
separately and remains concealed from Haru and Emi. He observes their
journey and the Kamakura proceedings. He retains his bow while Haru
surrenders the village-owned travel weapon at the compound. Takeshi
finds concealed observation positions around the Kamakura compound. He
learns that Truk knows villages are suffering and refuses mercy anyway.
He witnesses Haru and Emi being sentenced. At the Execution Courtyard,
Takeshi can no longer remain merely an observer. He pulls out his bow
and prepares to intervene. That movement exposes him to a guard. He is
chased and forced to flee. He survives and returns to Aokawa weeks
later. He reports that Haru and Emi reached Kamakura, Truk heard their
petition, and they were killed. He withholds the fuller details of the
audience, his attempted intervention, and the execution. Midori notices
that he is withholding something. Takeshi returns without Haru’s
village-owned weapon, which remained at the Kamakura compound. The
Village Chief is later angry about the loss of valuable communal
property.

CHAPTER 8 HANDOFF Takeshi begins Chapter 8 carrying unresolved guilt and
shame. He later kills Truk. Afterward he leaves Aokawa in shame. His
later life develops into the legend of the Gray Samurai, a Japanese
Robin-Hood-like folk hero. Do not depict Takeshi as the Gray Samurai in
Chapter 7. Do not add Gray Samurai insignia, title, costume, or explicit
legend foreshadowing unless a later script requires it.

LANGUAGE AND JAPANESE TEXT Takeshi speaks Japanese diegetically;
dialogue is rendered primarily in English with selected familiar
Japanese terms. He may address his father as Otou-san. The kana spelling
たけし is valid Japanese and may be used when the script or production
design calls for his name in Japanese. NO FAKE JAPANESE SCRIPT: Any
visible text that appears to be Japanese must be genuine, valid
Japanese. If valid wording has not been established, omit the writing
rather than inventing Japanese-looking glyphs.

PROHIBITED DRIFT - No mature adult appearance; Takeshi is 17 in Chapter
7. - No height substantially different from 5’7” except incidental
perspective. - No facial hair. - No blond, brown, white, or
fantasy-colored hair. - No pointed ears. - No samurai armor. - Do not
canonically identify him as a ninja. - No professional military
uniform. - No modern tactical gear, synthetic-looking webbing, zippers,
plastic buckles, or modern backpack design. - No firearms. - No fantasy
weapons or magical equipment. - No automatic aura or glowing eyes. - Do
not depict Takeshi as knowing or practicing magic. - Do not portray his
attempt to save Haru and Emi as cowardice; he actually prepares to
intervene. - Do not portray his escape as indifference. - Do not reveal
the Gray Samurai identity/title in Chapter 7. - No fake or
pseudo-Japanese glyphs anywhere in artwork.

RENDERING PRIORITY When detail must be simplified, preserve in this
order: 1. Youthful 17-year-old angular face and serious dark eyes 2.
Messy black topknot with loose strands 3. Large dark scarf/neck wrap 4.
5’7” lean athletic build 5. Dark patterned indigo/blue layered travel
clothing 6. Yumi and quiver 7. Wrapped lower legs and waraji sandals 8.
Traditional rope, water gourd, knife, and limited belt pouches 9. Quiet,
observant body language and youthful guilt/resolve

VISUAL STYLE IAKESI target style: approximately 70% photorealistic / 30%
manga. Takeshi should look like a capable seventeen-year-old mountain
tracker with a strong pre-modern stealth silhouette, not a modern action
hero. His clothing and equipment may accumulate dirt, snow, rain, and
wear during the journey. Maintain the same face, hair, age, build,
scarf, and core clothing throughout Chapters 7 and 8 unless the script
explicitly establishes a later change.
