WORLD OBSERVATION AREA (WOA) OBSERVATION STATION — CANONICAL PRODUCTION
SPECIFICATION

PURPOSE This specification defines the canonical World Observation Area
observation station used by Zorna, Nyssara, and Terra in the Domain of
the Gods.

The larger World Observation Area environment may vary between scenes.
The observation station itself is the persistent continuity element.

The canonical station consists of: - One magically floating 2-D
viewscreen. - One immediate floor/platform area beneath the screen. -
Concentric luminous circles on the floor. - No physical screen-support
apparatus.

The viewscreen and the immediate floor beneath it should remain
recognizably consistent whenever this observation station appears.

GENERAL ENVIRONMENT

The World Observation Area is part of the Domain of the Gods.

The larger surrounding architecture is not required to remain identical
from appearance to appearance.

It may include: - Tall divine architecture. - Columns. - Open luminous
space. - Dark polished surfaces. - Blue crystalline or luminous
architectural accents. - Gold or warm metallic accents. - Distant DOTG
structures.

However, the environment must not overwhelm the canonical observation
station.

The persistent visual identity comes from the floating blue viewscreen
and the concentric-circle floor treatment directly beneath it.

ART STYLE

Render the World Observation Area in the canonical Domain of the Gods
near-photorealistic illustration style.

The environment should feel materially convincing, elegant, and divine.

Avoid generic manga-background rendering for the DOTG.

VIEWSCREEN

Nature The viewscreen is magical.

It is not a television, monitor, holographic machine, projection device,
portal frame, or mechanical apparatus.

The viewscreen is a perfectly flat 2-D image plane suspended magically
in mid-air.

It has no physical thickness.

It has no physical support.

It has no projector.

It has no pedestal.

It has no mounting hardware.

It has no cables.

It has no visible technological mechanism.

Edge-On Behavior The viewscreen should be treated as geometrically 2-D.

When viewed exactly edge-on, it effectively disappears.

There is no meaningful side profile.

Do not depict a thick screen edge.

Do not depict a side-view support structure.

A dedicated side-view reference is unnecessary.

Screen Orientation The viewscreen normally floats vertically above the
canonical floor platform.

It should remain upright and stable unless a script explicitly specifies
otherwise.

Screen Shape Large horizontal rectangle.

The proportions should remain consistent across appearances.

Approximate production dimensions: Screen width: approximately 10
meters. Screen height: approximately 6 meters.

These are visual-scale guidelines rather than engineering measurements.

The important requirement is that the screen is large enough for
multiple Gods to stand before it and comfortably observe detailed
scenes.

Floating Height The bottom edge of the viewscreen floats approximately
1.2 meters above the floor.

Maintain a visually consistent relationship between the screen and the
concentric-circle floor area.

SCREEN BORDER

The screen has a luminous blue magical border.

The border is part of the magical image projection.

It is not a physical frame.

The border should be: - Bright blue. - Clean. - Crystalline or
energy-like. - Thin enough not to dominate the displayed image. -
Consistent in general thickness and glow.

The border may include restrained corner emphasis or small geometric
energy accents.

However, it must not contain: - A central apex crest. - A compass
symbol. - A multi-point star. - A starburst. - A magic-circle emblem. -
Runes. - Writing. - Unspecified religious or mystical symbols.

Keep the border visually elegant and simple.

SCREEN CONTENT

The content displayed by the viewscreen may change freely.

It may show: - Mortal cities. - Villages. - Landscapes. - Individuals. -
Historical events. - Distant worlds. - Geographic regions. - Close
views. - Wide views.

The displayed image is not part of the persistent prop design.

Do not add permanent interface text, menus, status bars, icons,
targeting marks, runes, or data overlays unless explicitly required by
the script.

The screen should normally display the observed scene cleanly.

SCREEN LIGHT

The screen emits a soft blue glow.

This glow may illuminate nearby characters and the floor slightly.

Do not allow the screen glow to: - Blow out faces. - Turn characters
entirely blue. - Destroy costume colors. - Crush surrounding shadows. -
Overpower the displayed image.

The screen should remain readable in CMYK-oriented production.

FLOOR / PLATFORM AREA

Nature The floor beneath the viewscreen is a persistent canonical part
of the observation station.

It is not a raised mechanical platform.

It is the immediate floor area visually associated with the screen.

The floor surface may be dark polished stone or another dark refined
DOTG material.

It should provide strong visual contrast with the luminous blue circles.

Shape The canonical floor treatment is circular.

Approximate diameter: Approximately 16 meters.

This is a production-scale guideline.

CONCENTRIC CIRCLES

The floor contains multiple luminous blue concentric circles centered
beneath the viewscreen.

These circles are the primary persistent floor design.

The circles should be: - Simple. - Clean. - Evenly centered. - Blue. -
Luminous. - Geometrically readable.

The floor pattern contains NO compass design.

It contains NO starburst.

It contains NO central emblem.

It contains NO magic circle.

It contains NO runes.

It contains NO writing.

It contains NO radial mystical symbol.

It contains NO decorative glyphs.

Concentric circles alone are sufficient.

CENTER OF FLOOR

The center of the concentric-circle pattern may contain a small
concentrated point or glow of blue light.

This is a magical visual effect, not a symbol.

It is not a projector.

It is not a device.

It is not a physical object.

It should remain simple and unobtrusive.

Do not turn the center glow into a star, compass, sigil, rune, or
mechanical emitter.

PHYSICAL SUPPORTS

There are no physical supports for the viewscreen.

FORBIDDEN: - Pedestals. - Stands. - Columns holding the screen. -
Brackets. - Frames extending to the floor. - Projection towers. -
Machinery. - Control consoles physically supporting the screen. -
Cables. - Mounting hardware.

The screen simply floats.

CONTROLS

The observation station does not require a permanent physical control
console.

Gods may manipulate the screen magically through gestures or other
script-defined actions.

Do not invent buttons, keyboards, touchscreens, levers, control desks,
or mechanical interfaces unless explicitly required by a script.

CHARACTER USE AND BLOCKING

The station is intended for multiple Gods to stand around and observe
the screen.

Zorna, Nyssara, and Terra commonly use this station.

Characters may stand: - To either side of the screen. - In front of the
screen. - On the concentric-circle floor area. - Slightly outside the
circle area when composition requires it.

The station should provide sufficient open space for three or more
characters without forcing them into unnatural crowding.

Character positions are scene-specific and should be defined by SBL
blocking rather than permanently encoded into the prop.

The floor circles should not be treated as mandatory standing marks.

They are environmental design, not character slots.

PERSISTENCE RULE

The larger WOA may vary.

The following must remain persistent: 1. Large horizontal floating 2-D
viewscreen. 2. Bright blue magical border. 3. No physical thickness. 4.
No physical support. 5. Screen disappears when viewed exactly edge-on.
6. Dark immediate floor/platform area. 7. Circular floor treatment
beneath screen. 8. Simple blue concentric luminous circles. 9. Small
optional central blue glow. 10. General scale relationship between
screen and floor.

LIGHTING

The WOA uses Domain of the Gods ambient lighting.

The viewscreen contributes localized blue illumination.

Lighting should preserve: - Character faces. - Costume colors. -
Environmental detail. - Dark-area detail. - CMYK printability.

Avoid: - Crushed blacks. - Excessively dark backgrounds. - Blinding
screen glow. - Extreme bloom. - Lens flare. - Harsh spotlight effects.

The station should look luminous without becoming visually noisy.

MATERIALS AND COLORS

Viewscreen Border: Divine luminous blue.

Floor: Dark polished stone or similarly refined dark DOTG material.

Floor Circles: Luminous blue.

Optional Floor-Center Glow: Bright blue-white core fading to blue.

Environmental Accents: Blue crystalline light and restrained warm gold
may appear in the surrounding WOA architecture.

The station itself should remain dominated by the contrast between the
dark floor and blue magical illumination.

2.  The viewscreen has no physical thickness.
3.  The viewscreen floats in mid-air.
4.  The viewscreen has no physical support apparatus.
5.  Viewed exactly edge-on, the viewscreen disappears.
6.  No dedicated physical side profile exists.
7.  The screen remains a large horizontal rectangle.
8.  Screen proportions remain consistent.
9.  The luminous border remains blue.
10. The border is magical, not physical.
11. Screen content may change freely.
12. No permanent UI or interface overlays appear.
13. The immediate floor treatment remains circular.
14. The floor uses simple luminous blue concentric circles.
15. No compass symbol appears on the floor.
16. No starburst appears on the floor.
17. No runes or writing appear on the floor.
18. No central emblem appears on the floor.
19. The center may contain only a simple blue glow.
20. No mechanical projector or control apparatus appears.
21. The larger WOA architecture may vary.
22. The viewscreen and immediate floor/platform remain the persistent
    continuity island.

FORBIDDEN ELEMENTS

Do not add: - Physical screen supports. - Screen stands. - Pedestals. -
Projection columns. - Mounting hardware. - Cables. - Projectors. -
Mechanical devices. - Thick screen edges. - A visible side-profile
structure. - Permanent control consoles. - Keyboards. - Buttons. -
Levers. - Generic computer interfaces. - Compass roses. - Multi-point
starbursts. - Magic circles. - Runes. - Writing on the screen border. -
Writing on the floor. - Central floor emblems. - Apex crests above the
screen. - Generic mystical symbols. - Permanent decorative symbols
shared with other story arcs. - Excessive bloom or glare.

SBL PRODUCTION NOTE

The observation station should be represented as a persistent
prop/environment assembly rather than reconstructed from a generic World
Observation Area description.

Recommended semantic components:

Environment: World_Observation_Area

Persistent Assembly: WOA_Observation_Station

Components: WOA_Viewscreen WOA_Floor_Area WOA_Concentric_Circles
WOA_Center_Glow

WOA_Viewscreen properties: - type = magical_2d_image_plane - orientation
= vertical - thickness = zero / visually nonexistent - support = none -
border = luminous_blue - content = scene-dependent

WOA_Floor_Area properties: - material = dark_polished_surface - shape =
circular - pattern = concentric_circles_only

WOA_Concentric_Circles properties: - color = luminous_blue - symbols =
none - writing = none - runes = none

The larger WOA environment should not be inherited as a rigid
architectural set unless a specific scene requires it.

AUTHORITY RULE

Use the canonical WOA observation-station production-sheet artwork, the
approved Chapter 1 reference page showing Zorna, Nyssara, and Terra
using the screen, and this textual specification together.

If a generated production-sheet detail conflicts with this
specification, the explicit textual rules are authoritative for: -
Screen dimensionality. - Absence of physical support. - Edge-on
disappearance. - Floor geometry. - Concentric-circle-only pattern. -
Absence of compass/star symbols. - Absence of mechanical apparatus. -
Persistence of the screen and immediate floor area.

The canonical WOA viewscreen is a floating, perfectly flat magical 2-D
image plane.

The canonical WOA floor pattern is simple concentric luminous circles
and nothing more.
