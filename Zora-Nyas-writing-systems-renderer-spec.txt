IAKESI ZORA AND NYAS WRITING SYSTEMS RENDERER SPECIFICATION

Purpose
This file is the prompt-facing production reference for visible Zoran and Nyassarian writing in manga artwork. Use it with Zora-Nyas-writing-systems-sheet.png. The scripts are decorative fictional systems, not encodings of English dialogue.

GLOBAL RULES
- Never use recognizable real-world letters or writing systems.
- Do not imitate Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, Arabic, Hebrew, Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Devanagari, Egyptian, Mayan, or other historical scripts.
- Never substitute pseudo-Japanese or pseudo-Chinese characters.
- Keep each civilization's script visually consistent across signs, books, documents, maps, monuments, and diagrams.
- Visible reader dialogue remains ordinary comic lettering; these systems are for diegetic writing inside the depicted world.
- Do not ask an image renderer to spell English with either fictional script. Generate plausible decorative glyph sequences instead.

NYASSARIAN SCRIPT
Identity
Nyassarian writing represents science, observation, engineering, printing, and accumulated knowledge.

Direction and spacing
- Write in vertical columns from top to bottom.
- Order columns from right to left.
- Separate word groups with a small divider mark rather than blank spaces.

Glyph construction
- Use precise engineered pictographs.
- Build glyphs from straight stems, wedges, hooks, loops, enclosed squares or circles, dots, and short crossbars.
- Keep strokes measured, drafted, engraved, and mechanically repeatable.
- Avoid expressive brushwork and calligraphic flourishes.

Historical forms
- Classical manuscripts use more ornate versions of the same glyph families.
- Printed and movable-type forms are simplified and standardized but visibly related to the classical forms.
- Stone inscriptions use heavier, durable versions of the same construction logic.

Numbers and technical notation
- Use twelve dedicated numeral symbols in a base-12 system.
- Numerals must not resemble Arabic digits.
- Scientific material may combine Nyassarian glyphs with restrained measurement ticks, arrows, construction grids, and diagram marks.

Nyassarian prompt block
Use the canonical Nyassarian script: entirely fictional scientific writing made from precise engineered pictographic glyphs using stems, wedges, hooks, loops, enclosed geometric forms, dots, and short crossbars. Write it in vertical top-to-bottom columns ordered right-to-left, with small divider marks between word groups. Use distinct fictional base-12 numerals. Make the lettering look carefully drafted, engraved, or set in movable type. Do not use or resemble any real-world script.

ZORAN SCRIPT
Identity
Zoran writing is a decorative engineered fantasy script associated with Ashiba's design tradition under Zorna's guidance.

Direction and spacing
- Write horizontally from left to right.
- Separate words with normal blank spaces.
- Use typical word groups of three to eight glyphs.
- Do not use uppercase or lowercase forms.

Glyph construction
- Build glyphs from circles, squares, triangles, hollow pentagons, diamonds, hexagons, four-point stars, and thin stacked rectangles.
- Combine and modify these shapes to expand the glyph inventory.
- Keep line weight clean, geometric, orderly, and easy for artists to reproduce.

Context variants
- Temple inscriptions are large, formal, and spacious.
- Books use short decorative titles.
- Official documents use a title, compact text blocks, and an appropriate canonical seal.
- Spell scrolls use dense but orderly decorative sequences.
- Avoid repeating identical decorative strings in adjacent panels.

Numbers punctuation and currency
- Use ten dedicated decimal numeral symbols independent of the alphabet.
- Support a geometric period, comma, colon, semicolon, question mark, and exclamation mark.
- Use a crystal-inspired currency symbol for the Lumen.
- Preserve canonical symbols separately: Zoran Empire crest, Temple emblem, Imperial Magic Academy crest, Goddess of Light emblem, Mana Crystal icon, Staff of Eternal Light icon, elemental magic icons, and Demon Empire emblem.

Zoran prompt block
Use the canonical Zoran script: an entirely fictional left-to-right decorative geometric writing system with ordinary spaces between three-to-eight-glyph word groups and no uppercase or lowercase. Construct glyphs from circles, squares, triangles, hollow pentagons, diamonds, hexagons, four-point stars, and thin stacked rectangles. Use clean uniform strokes, distinct fictional decimal numerals, geometric punctuation, and a crystal-inspired Lumen currency mark. Do not encode English and do not use or resemble any real-world script.

SELECTION RULE
- Use Nyassarian for Nyas books, scientific papers, maps, printing, engineering labels, scholarly inscriptions, and technical diagrams.
- Use Zoran for Zora temple inscriptions, books, official documents, spell scrolls, seals, currency notation, and magical institutions.
