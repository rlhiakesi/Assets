JEREMIEL CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION
IAKESI Revised Edition

PURPOSE
This file is the binding textual reference for Jeremiel in Chapter 4 and later appearances. Use it together with Jeremiel-character-sheet.png and Jeremiel-expression-sheet.png. If an incidental rendering conflicts with this specification, this specification controls unless the script explicitly establishes a temporary change.

IDENTITY
Name: Jeremiel
Race: Zoran
Sex: Male
Age during Chapter 4: 63
Height: Approximately 5 feet 10 inches (178 cm)
Build: Lean, slightly angular, and naturally dignified; neither frail nor muscular
Ears: Human ears only; never pointed or elven
Status: Retired senior councilman
Former public role: Statesman and senior member of the Zoran Council
Continuing roles: Scholar, natural philosopher, magical researcher, and teacher
Residence: Near the Royal Assembly in the capital of Zora
Contemporary nicknames: Old Six Schools; The Prophet of Tomorrow

STORY FUNCTION
Jeremiel is a respected former member of the governing institution, not an unknown outsider. His former Council service explains his access to the chamber, his familiarity with its members, and the Council's familiarity with his repeated arguments. Their mockery is institutional and personal: they know him, respect his history, and believe his six-school theory is an eccentric fixation. His eventual vindication therefore carries greater weight.

PERSONALITY
Jeremiel is quiet, patient, deeply curious, logical, humble, compassionate, honest, and principled. He is never vindictive. He prefers understanding, evidence, and instruction over confrontation. Mockery does not provoke him into anger. He can issue a grave warning without becoming theatrical or self-important. His kindness remains visible even when he is exhausted or afraid.

FACE AND HEAD
Apparent age: Early sixties
Face: Lean and mature, with defined cheekbones, gentle eyes, deep smile lines, and restrained age lines
Eyes: Warm gray to amber-brown; never glowing unless a script explicitly describes a temporary magical effect
Hair: White, short-to-medium length, loosely layered and slightly tousled
Forelock: One persistent forelock falls between or near the eyes and must remain recognizable across views
Facial hair: Short white mustache connected to a narrow, pointed chin beard
Cheeks: No full cheek beard
Default expression: Thoughtful, humane, observant, and quietly determined

SILHOUETTE
Jeremiel must remain recognizable when his face is small. His binding silhouette consists of:
- asymmetrical ivory sleeveless scholar's mantle;
- dark high-collared robe;
- diagonal leather harness;
- rigid cylindrical scroll case carried across his back;
- small six-circle brass clasp at the throat;
- lean scholarly posture rather than the rigid bearing of an active official.

CLOTHING
Inner garment: Very dark midnight-blue high-collared scholar's robe, appearing nearly black in deep shadow
Outer garment: Asymmetrical ivory sleeveless mantle with restrained antique-gold embroidery and tassel details
Harness: Worn brown leather, diagonally crossing the torso and supporting the scroll case
Waist: Layered practical belts suitable for notebooks, ink, and small research tools
Boots: Practical, worn, dark brown scholar's travel boots
Condition: Well maintained but visibly used; scholarly rather than wealthy or ceremonial
Official distinction: Jeremiel does not wear a current Council uniform
Forbidden insignia: No Council sun or star emblem, no large emblem on the back, and no obsolete wheel-shaped Six Schools emblem

SIX SCHOOLS CLASP
The small brass clasp at Jeremiel's throat is the canonical Six Schools symbol.
Construction: Exactly six linked circular settings
Five circles: Each contains a small abstract symbol representing an established school
Sixth circle: Completely empty until a script explicitly requires a later altered form
Meaning: Knowledge is incomplete; an empty place must remain for what has not yet been discovered
Rendering rule: The clasp must not become a seven-circle device, a wheel, a Council emblem, a compass rose, or a magical halo

ACCESSORIES
Primary accessory: Rigid cylindrical scroll case worn diagonally across the back
Common carried items: Scrolls, notebook, writing instruments, ink, and small belt pouches
Hands: Two or more fingertips commonly carry visible ink stains
Weapons: None by default
Staff or wand: None

COLOR PALETTE
Hair: White, approximately #E5E4E2
Eyes: Warm gray or amber-brown
Skin: Natural warm complexion appropriate to the character sheet
Main robe: Very dark midnight blue, approximately #151A2A; nearly black in shadow
Mantle: Warm ivory, approximately #EDE6D6
Embroidery: Antique gold, approximately #C49A3A
Clasp: Aged brass, approximately #8B6B3C
Harness and boots: Worn dark brown leather, approximately #3A2D21

EXPRESSIONS
Jeremiel's expressions remain controlled and mature. Canonical expressions include:
- neutral kindness;
- thoughtful concentration;
- patient instruction;
- quiet warning;
- restrained amusement;
- tolerant skepticism;
- startled fear;
- grave concern;
- exhausted determination;
- scholarly excitement;
- gentle smile;
- firm resolve.
Avoid exaggerated slapstick distortion, wild rage, villainous smirking, or youthful anime affectations.

POSTURE AND MOVEMENT
Jeremiel stands with a slightly relaxed scholarly posture. He may lean forward when explaining an idea or examining evidence. His gestures are economical and purposeful: raising one finger, opening a notebook, presenting a chart, or patiently waiting for a room to quiet. He does not pose like a warrior, monarch, or active government official.

CHAPTER 4 CONTINUITY
Chapter 4 occurs over approximately one to three months. Jeremiel does not visibly age during the chapter. Keep his hair length, beard length, facial age, costume construction, and core accessories consistent. Temporary fatigue, dust, snow, ink, or clothing wear may vary when supported by the scene.

PROHIBITED DRIFT
- No pointed or elven ears.
- No blond, brown, or gray-brown hair.
- No long tied hair or ponytail.
- No full cheek beard.
- No muscular or heroic-warrior physique.
- No active Council uniform.
- No crown, armor, weapon, staff, or wand.
- No Council sun or star insignia.
- No large back emblem.
- No old wheel-shaped Six Schools emblem.
- No more or fewer than six circles in the canonical clasp.
- No symbol in the empty sixth circle unless explicitly required by the script.
- No automatic magical aura or glowing eyes.
- Do not depict him as an unknown outsider to the Council.
- Do not state that his warnings went unheard.
- Do not claim that he personally invented mana-crystal refining methods.
- Do not add claims about his death or the later Temple of Light without explicit script authority.

RENDERING PRIORITY
When detail must be simplified, preserve in this order:
1. White layered hair with the persistent forelock and pointed beard
2. Human ears and mature lean face
3. Asymmetrical ivory mantle over the midnight-blue robe
4. Diagonal scroll-case harness
5. Six-circle brass clasp with one empty circle
6. Ink-stained fingers and practical scholarly accessories
