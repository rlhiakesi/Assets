TRUK CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION IAKESI Revised Edition

PURPOSE This file is the binding textual reference for Truk in Chapters
7 and 8. Use it together with Truk-character-reference.png and
Truk-expressions.png. If incidental artwork conflicts with this
specification, this specification controls unless the script explicitly
establishes a temporary change.

IDENTITY Name: Truk Race/Nationality: Earth human; Japanese Sex: Male
Age during Chapter 7: Approximately 50–60 Height: Approximately 5 feet 8
inches / 173 cm Build: Solid, mature, well-kept Role: Senior Kamakura
official with authority to hear the Aokawa petition Home/Location:
Kamakura Status at end of Chapter 7: Alive Chapter 8 fate: Killed by
Takeshi

STORY FUNCTION Truk is the principal human antagonist of Chapter 7. He
represents abusive authority rather than a misunderstood or benevolent
administrator. Before Haru and Emi arrive, Truk already knows that rural
villages are suffering severe shortages. He nevertheless insists that
obligations be collected. Haru and Emi therefore confront him with
suffering he has already chosen to disregard.

Truk hears their petition and refuses relief. When Haru points out the
practical consequence of stripping a starving village of its remaining
resources, Truk treats the challenge to his judgment and authority as
defiance. He orders Haru and Emi executed without a meaningful trial or
appeal.

Truk survives Chapter 7. Takeshi kills him in Chapter 8.

PERSONALITY Truk is intelligent, analytical, observant, authoritarian,
disciplined, calculating, arrogant, dismissive, politically aware, and
deeply conscious of hierarchy. He values order, procedure, control, and
institutional stability above the welfare of powerless villagers. He is
calm in public and does not need theatrical rage to be cruel.

Do not soften Truk into a basically fair official trapped by the system.
He knowingly understands that villages are suffering and chooses
enforcement anyway. He does not need to hate peasants personally; he
regards their suffering as subordinate to order and authority.

FACE AND HEAD Apparent age: Approximately 50–60 Face: Mature, angular,
stern, with sharp eyes Eyes: Dark brown Hair: Black, worn beneath formal
court headwear Facial hair: Trim mustache and goatee Default expression:
Cold, controlled, judgmental Ears: Normal human ears No supernatural
features

BODY AND SILHOUETTE Height: Approximately 5’8” / 173 cm Build: Solid and
well-kept, not oversized or warrior-muscular Binding silhouette: -
formal black court headwear / eboshi-style cap; - dark layered Kamakura
official clothing; - restrained antique-gold detailing; - broad formal
sleeves; - upright controlled posture. His authority should come from
bearing, setting, clothing, and the behavior of subordinates rather than
physical intimidation.

CLOTHING Primary attire: Formal dark Kamakura-period
administrative/court clothing Main colors: Black, charcoal, very dark
indigo Trim/pattern: Restrained antique-gold patterning Inner robe: Dark
muted brown/indigo Headwear: Formal black court cap Footwear:
Period-appropriate formal footwear Condition: Immaculate and well
maintained Overall impression: Senior administrative authority, not
battlefield warrior

The approved design preserves the original Truk face and black/gold
silhouette while removing unsupported bureaucratic lore and invented
Japanese labeling.

OFFICE AND TITLE The story requires Truk to be a powerful Kamakura
official capable of hearing the petition and abusing his authority.
Production-facing labels should use “Truk — Senior Official,” “Truk —
Kamakura Official,” or similarly neutral English wording. Do NOT use the
earlier invented title “Gokushō Bugyō.” Do NOT use 御家人奉行 as Truk’s
title. Do NOT claim that “Senior Magistrate of Petitions” is his
historical office. Do NOT state that he was directly appointed by the
Shōgun unless a later script explicitly establishes it. Do not build
story logic around a precise historical bureaucratic title that has not
been established.

EQUIPMENT AND PROPS Default character appearance: No weapon. Petition
scroll/document: Permitted when required by the scene. Administrative
documents: Permitted. Brush and ink: Permitted if valid scene content is
established. Official seals: Only if the actual Japanese seal text has
been deliberately established and validated. No modern objects. No
fantasy or magical equipment.

EXPRESSIONS Canonical expressions include: - neutral; - dismissive; -
contemptuous; - thinking; - irritated; - angry; - speaking; - cold; -
curious; - calculating; - satisfied; - weary; - disapproving; -
skeptical; - commanding. Truk normally remains controlled. Avoid cartoon
villain laughter, manic rage, exaggerated sadism, or comedy expressions.

POSTURE AND MOVEMENT Truk’s posture is upright, deliberate, and
economical. He does not need to shout or wave his arms to command
attention. In the Audience Chamber, he listens from a position of
institutional power. His gestures should be small and decisive: looking
down at petitioners, reviewing a document, raising a hand to stop
discussion, issuing an order, or turning away after judgment.

RELATIONSHIPS Haru and Emi: Aokawa villagers who petition for relief.
Truk rejects them despite knowing rural conditions are severe and orders
their execution after Haru challenges his judgment. Midori: Truk does
not personally interact with Midori in Chapter 7. Takeshi: Secretly
observes the proceedings. Takeshi later kills Truk in Chapter 8.
Kamakura Messenger/Aide: Officials or subordinates who provide
information and carry out administrative duties. Aokawa: Truk has no
personal connection to the village beyond his administrative authority.

CHAPTER 7 CONTINUITY Before the Aokawa petition, a Kamakura messenger
communicates that Kamakura’s obligations are to be met in full. An aide
warns Truk that several mountain villages are already reporting
shortages. Truk dismisses the concern and orders collection of what is
owed. Haru and Emi later reach Kamakura after traveling for weeks. They
wait for permission to present their petition. They enter the Kamakura
Audience Chamber and address Truk respectfully. Haru and Emi explain
Aokawa’s crisis and ask for relief. Truk already knows rural communities
are suffering. Emi asks that the burden be reduced for one year and uses
the respectful plea “Onegaishimasu.” Truk refuses. Haru argues that if
Truk takes what little remains, there will be nothing left to tax the
following year. Truk treats this as insolence and defiance. He orders
Haru and Emi executed. No meaningful trial or appeal follows. Takeshi
witnesses the sequence and later attempts to intervene at the execution.
Truk remains alive at the end of Chapter 7.

CHAPTER 8 HANDOFF Takeshi carries guilt and anger from Haru and Emi’s
deaths. Takeshi later kills Truk in Chapter 8. Do not kill, imprison,
remove, or redeem Truk during Chapter 7. Do not give Truk remorse that
would undermine the Chapter 8 confrontation unless the script explicitly
requires it.

JAPANESE LANGUAGE AND TEXT Haru and Emi may address Truk as Truk-sama
where appropriate to Japanese social hierarchy. NO FAKE JAPANESE SCRIPT.
Any visible Japanese writing associated with Truk—documents, seals,
signs, calligraphy, labels, decrees, architecture, or props—must be
genuine valid Japanese. If valid Japanese wording has not been
deliberately established, omit the writing rather than generating
Japanese-looking pseudo-glyphs. Do not reuse decorative calligraphy or
seal text from older concept art merely because it looks plausible. Do
not add Japanese titles to the character sheet unless they have been
explicitly validated.

PROHIBITED DRIFT - Do not depict Truk as a bandit, outlaw, ninja, or
mountain warrior. - Do not depict him as a battlefield samurai. - No fur
cloak, ragged bandit clothing, survivalist gear, or exposed muscular
warrior styling. - No invented exact historical office title presented
as fact. - No “Gokushō Bugyō.” - No 御家人奉行 title. - No unsupported
claim that he is directly appointed by the Shōgun. - Do not portray him
as fundamentally fair, altruistic, or secretly sympathetic to Aokawa. -
Do not make him a cackling theatrical sadist. - No default sword as part
of his defining silhouette. - No fantasy armor, magical aura, glowing
eyes, staff, wand, or supernatural power. - No modern clothing or
equipment. - No fake or pseudo-Japanese writing. - Do not kill Truk in
Chapter 7. - Do not remove his responsibility for Haru and Emi’s
execution.

RENDERING PRIORITY When detail must be simplified, preserve in this
order: 1. Mature stern face with trim mustache and goatee 2. Formal
black court headwear 3. Dark formal Kamakura official robes 4.
Restrained antique-gold trim/patterning 5. Upright controlled
authoritative posture 6. Cold dismissive/judgmental expression 7.
Period-appropriate administrative environment and props 8. No default
weapon

VISUAL STYLE IAKESI target style: approximately 70% photorealistic / 30%
manga. Truk should look imposing because he possesses institutional
authority, not because he resembles a fantasy warlord. Preserve the
strong face and dark formal silhouette of the original design while
removing unsupported bureaucratic lore, questionable Japanese labels,
fake calligraphy, seals, and default weaponry.
