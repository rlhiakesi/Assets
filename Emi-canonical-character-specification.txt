EMI CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION IAKESI Revised Edition

PURPOSE This file is the binding textual reference for Emi in Chapter 7.
Use it together with the approved Emi-character-sheet.png and
Emi-expression-sheet.png. If an incidental rendering conflicts with this
specification, this specification controls unless the script explicitly
establishes a temporary change.

IDENTITY Name: Emi (恵美) Race/Nationality: Earth human; Japanese Sex:
Female Age during Chapter 7: 33 Height: 5 feet 4 inches (163 cm) Build:
Slim, healthy, naturally proportioned adult woman; practical
working-village build Eyes: Warm dark brown Hair: Black Role: Midori’s
mother; Haru’s wife; Aokawa villager Historical setting: Kamakura-era
Japan Status at beginning of Chapter 7: Living human Status at end of
Chapter 7: Deceased on Earth; chooses Door 3 and becomes an Acolyte

STORY FUNCTION Emi is Midori’s mother and Haru’s equal partner. She
establishes the loving family Midori loses, participates actively in
Aokawa’s attempt to survive famine and taxation, and independently
chooses to accompany Haru to Kamakura to petition for relief. Her death
helps define Midori’s Chapter 8 circumstances. In the Soul Relocation
Area, Emi again acts independently: she is the first of the couple to
state that she wants to remain in the Dimension of the Gods, after which
Haru agrees and they choose to become Acolytes together.

PERSONALITY Emi is kind, nurturing, resilient, thoughtful, patient,
practical, resourceful, compassionate, and quietly courageous. She is
deeply devoted to Haru and Midori but is not passive or subordinate to
Haru. She makes her own decisions and accepts hardship without
theatrical martyrdom. Her emotional strength is expressed through
restraint, care, and persistence rather than aggression.

FACE AND HEAD Apparent age: Early thirties Face: Warm, mature but
youthful, with gentle adult features Eyes: Warm dark brown; never
glowing unless a script explicitly establishes a temporary effect Hair:
Black, usually gathered into a practical loose bun or updo Loose
strands: Several natural loose strands may frame the face Complexion:
Natural warm complexion; light natural freckles may be visible Default
expression: Warm, attentive, composed, and quietly concerned Rendering
rule: Maintain consistent facial geometry throughout Chapter 7

BODY AND SILHOUETTE Canonical height is 5 feet 4 inches / 163 cm. Emi
must not be vertically stretched or given fashion-model proportions.
Binding silhouette: - modest adult female proportions; - practical black
hair bun with loose face-framing strands; - muted maroon/reddish-brown
kimono; - dark charcoal/slate obi; - rural footwear; - calm, grounded
posture. Leg and torso proportions must read as a 5’4” adult woman. She
is slim and healthy, not frail, unusually tall, exaggeratedly curvy, or
stylized as a fantasy heroine.

CLOTHING — EARTH / AOKAWA Primary garment: Muted reddish-brown to maroon
patterned kimono Pattern: Restrained floral or woven motif appropriate
to a rural Kamakura-era setting Inner collar: Pale neutral fabric
Obi/sash: Dark charcoal or slate, practical rather than ceremonial
Footwear: Practical rural straw sandals or the established equivalent
Condition: Clean but visibly used and appropriate to a household with
limited means Social presentation: Rural working mother, not nobility,
warrior, court lady, priestess, or fantasy character Jewelry: None by
default Armor: None Weapons: None by default

COLOR PALETTE Hair: Near-black, approximately #1A1A1A Eyes: Warm dark
brown, approximately #4A2E1E Skin: Natural warm fair-to-medium Japanese
complexion Kimono main: Muted maroon/reddish brown, approximately
#8B3A3A Kimono pattern: Muted dusty rose/tan, approximately #C9A898
Obi/sash: Charcoal/slate, approximately #3E3E46 Inner collar: Warm
ivory, approximately #E8E1D9 Footwear: Natural straw/brown

EXPRESSIONS Emi’s expressions are human, restrained, and mature.
Canonical useful expressions include: - neutral / calm; - gentle
smile; - warm happiness; - thoughtful; - concerned; - worried; - sad; -
determined; - compassionate; - loving toward Midori; - gentle
firmness; - surprised; - reflective / looking down; - profile left; -
profile right. Avoid exaggerated slapstick distortion, childish
affectations, glamorous posing, villainous expressions, or excessive
melodrama.

POSTURE AND MOVEMENT Emi moves like a practical village woman accustomed
to household and community work. Her gestures are economical and
natural: preparing food, carrying baskets, tending to Midori, walking
long distances, kneeling formally, or bowing respectfully. She does not
pose like a warrior, aristocrat, or model. During the journey to
Kamakura, fatigue and weather may affect posture without changing her
canonical proportions.

RELATIONSHIPS Haru: Husband and equal partner. Haru initially volunteers
to go to Kamakura; Emi independently insists that they will both go.
Midori: Daughter and central emotional priority. Midori addresses Emi as
Okaa-san. Emi supports Midori’s basic reading and writing instruction at
Aokawa’s local Buddhist temple. Takeshi: Trusted family friend and son
of the Village Chief. No romantic implication. Village Chief: Aokawa’s
local leader. Emi and Haru trust his assurance that Midori will be cared
for while they are away. Truk: Kamakura official who rejects the
petition and orders Haru and Emi executed. Terra: Earth’s Goddess. Once
Emi understands Terra’s divine status in the SRA, she naturally
addresses her as Terra-sama.

CHAPTER 7 CONTINUITY Aokawa is suffering from a failed harvest, depleted
stores, and severe resource/tax pressure following the recent
Mongol-invasion era. Emi and Haru reduce their own food and try to
protect Midori. Emi is present when Midori shares part of her own ration
with her. Emi and Haru participate in the village crisis and petition
decision. Haru says he will go to Kamakura; Emi independently answers
that they will both go. They leave Midori in Aokawa under the Village
Chief’s promised care. Emi and Haru travel to Kamakura on foot. The
journey lasts weeks through difficult mountainous country. They do not
ride horses. The Village Chief issues Haru a village-owned travel weapon
from communal stores. Emi does not carry a warrior’s weapon. At the
Kamakura compound, Haru surrenders the village weapon. Emi participates
actively in the petition before Truk. She asks that the burden be
reduced for one year and uses the respectful plea “Onegaishimasu.” Truk
orders Haru and Emi executed. Emi dies in Chapter 7.

SOUL RELOCATION AREA CONTINUITY At the moment of their deaths, Emi and
Haru arrive in the Soul Relocation Area (SRA). The SRA is a distinct
canonical DOTG environment and must never be confused with the World
Observation Area (WOA) or COG. Their arrival is an ordinary Earth-soul
arrival, not a special divine selection. Terra receives them as Earth’s
Goddess performing normal SRA duty.

The three standard choices presented to them are: 1. Return to Earth:
reincarnation as a baby in a new body; memories of the previous life are
lost. Any karmic relationship between prior hardship and later
circumstances remains deliberately unexplained. 2. Another world:
memories are retained; a new body is created corresponding to the
person’s Earth body at death. 3. Remain in the DOTG: the person becomes
an Acolyte and retains the existing spiritual form.

Emi chooses Door 3. She asks whether they will ever know what happened
to Midori. Terra explains that they cannot do so while they are
Acolytes. If they eventually become Neophytes, they will be able to look
backward through Earth time. Terra gives only a rough expectation of
“around a millennium, perhaps,” explicitly depending on their progress.
Chapter 7 does not further define the advancement system. Emi says,
“Then I want to remain.” Haru independently agrees. Together they choose
to become Acolytes. Emi and Haru are elsewhere when Midori later reaches
the SRA in Chapter 8. They do not reunite with Midori until much later
in the story.

LANGUAGE AND HONORIFICS Chapter 7 dialogue is English-translated
Japanese with selected familiar Japanese terms retained. Midori may
address Emi as Okaa-san. Emi may use Onegaishimasu in a formal plea.
Once Emi recognizes Terra as a Goddess, she addresses her as Terra-sama.
Japanese humans habitually use -sama when directly addressing a
recognized Goddess. Non-Japanese cultures use their established forms
such as Lady Terra instead.

PROHIBITED DRIFT - No tall or elongated fashion-model proportions. - No
height substantially above 5’4” unless a scene’s perspective creates an
incidental apparent difference. - No blond, brown, red, or
fantasy-colored hair. - No elaborate aristocratic hairstyle. - No noble,
courtly, warrior, shrine-maiden, or fantasy-priestess costume. - No
armor. - No personal sword or warrior equipment. - No jewelry by
default. - No magical aura, glowing eyes, or visible supernatural
ability while alive on Earth. - Do not depict Emi or other historical
Japanese humans as knowing or practicing magic. - No horse travel to
Kamakura. - Do not depict Emi as merely following Haru’s decision to
petition; she independently chooses to accompany him. - Do not depict
the Village Chief as forcing Emi to go. - Do not depict Haru and Emi as
specially selected for the SRA. - Do not imply that becoming an Acolyte
immediately allows Emi to observe, contact, guide, or reunite with
Midori. - Do not use later Acolyte clothing for living Emi in Aokawa. -
Do not introduce Chapter 19 costume details into this Chapter 7 Earth
reference.

RENDERING PRIORITY When detail must be simplified, preserve in this
order: 1. Emi’s warm early-thirties face and dark-brown eyes 2. Black
practical bun/updo with loose face-framing strands 3. Correct 5’4” adult
proportions; never elongated 4. Muted maroon/reddish-brown rural kimono
5. Dark charcoal/slate obi and pale inner collar 6. Practical rural
footwear and grounded working-village posture 7. Restrained,
compassionate emotional acting

VISUAL STYLE IAKESI target style: approximately 70% photorealistic / 30%
manga. Emi should remain attractive but ordinary and believable. Do not
glamorize poverty or convert her into a fantasy heroine. During travel,
her clothing may become dusty, wet, wrinkled, or worn when supported by
the scene, but the core design must remain recognizable. Execution
scenes should preserve dignity and avoid graphic gore.
