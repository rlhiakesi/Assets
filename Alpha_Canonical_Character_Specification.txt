ALPHA — CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION

Identity Alpha is the Supreme God and head of the Council of Gods.

He is the only being capable of creating entire worlds. He does not seek
worship or glory and normally intervenes only when necessary to preserve
creation and the divine order.

Council Position Alpha occupies the fixed 12:00 position at the Council
table.

There are seven ordinary voting Council members besides Alpha. Alpha
normally does not involve himself in routine Council voting because he
expects the seven members to produce a majority.

He votes or resolves a decision when an abstention, absence, recusal, or
similar circumstance prevents the ordinary Council from reaching a
decision.

Routine administrative work and unnecessary bureaucracy irritate him.

Apparent Age 80.

Alpha is actually far older than any existing world. Eighty is the
visual age target only.

Height Approximately 6 feet 4 inches (193 cm).

Build Broad-shouldered and physically powerful despite his apparent age.

Alpha must look old, but never frail. His body should communicate that
age has not diminished his fundamental physical presence.

Face Clearly elderly male facial proportions.

Deep but natural age lines, forehead lines, crow’s feet, weathered skin,
and aged hands are appropriate.

His face should communicate intelligence, patience, enormous experience,
and authority.

Avoid making him look merely middle-aged with white hair.

Avoid making him look sickly, weak, or decrepit.

Eyes Steel blue.

His resting eyes may appear tired, observant, or mildly bored.

When Alpha focuses on a person or situation, his gaze becomes extremely
direct and penetrating.

Skin Fair and weathered by apparent age.

Ears Normal human-shaped ears.

Neither pointed nor elongated. Never render Alpha with elf-like,
fae-like, or otherwise pointed ears.

Hair Pure white.

Very long, thick, and slightly wavy.

The hair is swept backward from the forehead with no bangs.

It reaches approximately to the shoulder blades.

The hair should remain less visually massive than the beard.

Do not give Alpha a center-parted youthful hairstyle, bangs, elaborate
braids, or ornamental hair accessories.

Beard Pure white.

Extremely full, thick, and long.

The beard reaches approximately to the upper waist.

It should remain broad and substantial toward the bottom rather than
tapering into a narrow stereotypical wizard point.

The beard is one of Alpha’s strongest identifying features and must
remain consistent.

Do not shorten it into an ordinary chest-length beard.

Do not make it excessively neat, sculpted, braided, jeweled, or
ornamented.

Default Outfit Alpha has one canonical outfit.

He does not maintain separate casual and formal wardrobes.

Use this outfit unless a script explicitly requires something
exceptional.

The outfit consists of: - Ivory/white inner robe. - Ivory/white outer
mantle. - Restrained warm-gold edging. - Broad simple warm-brown or
muted-gold belt. - Simple brown leather sandals.

The silhouette is long, heavy, simple, and monumental.

The robes should communicate authority through quality, scale, and
simplicity rather than decorative complexity.

Outfit Simplification Avoid complicated belt hardware.

Avoid dangling medallions, chains, multiple buckles, hanging cords,
tassels, or small accessories that are likely to drift between generated
images.

Gold trim should be restrained and repeatable.

The back of the robe should remain plain ivory/white with restrained
edging.

Do not place a large emblem on the back.

Jewelry None.

Alpha does not require jewelry to communicate rank.

Do not add crowns, tiaras, circlets, necklaces, rings, ornamental
chains, jeweled belts, or decorative magical artifacts unless explicitly
required by a script.

Symbols Alpha has no generic star, compass, magic-circle, or similar
emblem as part of his canonical appearance.

Never use the recurring multi-point star/compass motif commonly
introduced by AI-generated fantasy artwork.

If a specific Alpha or Council symbol is later designed, it must be
defined separately and intentionally.

Posture Upright, relaxed, and immensely self-assured.

Alpha does not pose theatrically to establish authority.

He may stand quietly with his arms relaxed, rest one hand near his belt,
stroke his beard while listening, or extend a hand when addressing
someone.

He should look like someone who assumes he will be obeyed because
experience has taught him that he will be.

Movement Slow, deliberate, and economical.

Alpha rarely makes unnecessary gestures.

He does not rush.

When he moves decisively, the action should carry weight precisely
because his normal movement is so controlled.

Presence Alpha does not normally radiate visible magical energy.

Do not surround him with a permanent aura, glow, lightning, floating
runes, magical particles, or other effects merely to communicate power.

His authority comes from physical presence, behavior, rank, and the
reactions of others.

Visible supernatural effects should become significant when Alpha
actually exercises extraordinary power, especially during world
creation.

Voice Deep, resonant, calm, and controlled.

Alpha rarely raises his voice.

He normally speaks only as loudly as necessary.

When Alpha speaks with full authority, others listen without requiring
theatrical shouting.

Personality Patient. Quiet. Observant. Extremely difficult to anger.
Wise. Pragmatic. Authoritative. Dry sense of humor. Values order,
balance, honesty, and competent decision-making. Willing to admit
mistakes. Dislikes pointless bureaucracy.

Alpha normally allows other Gods to govern, debate, and solve problems
themselves.

He does not micromanage.

When he does intervene, the consequences are significant.

Philosophy Power exists to preserve creation, not to glorify oneself.

Alpha believes divine civilization must continue to grow rather than
merely preserve and recycle what already exists.

He is willing to spend accumulated worship reserves to create something
valuable today rather than indefinitely postpone creation in order to
preserve the reserves.

Relationship with Terra Terra is Alpha’s trusted special assistant and
advisor.

Alpha values her judgment, institutional memory, and ability to
recognize solutions others overlook.

Their familiarity should be visible in their interactions. Terra can
speak to Alpha with considerably less ceremony than ordinary
subordinates.

Relationship with the Council Alpha is Council head but normally remains
above routine decision-making.

He expects the seven ordinary voting members to resolve ordinary
disagreements.

He finds it mildly irritating when their voting structure forces him to
resolve matters that he believes they should have settled themselves.

In Chapter 1, the first 3–3–1 deadlock produces irritation.

The second 3–3–1 deadlock produces a full facepalm/faceplant and the
line:

(Alpha: “…OMG.”)

This comedic reaction is canonical and should not undermine Alpha’s
authority. The humor works because someone of Alpha’s status is being
forced to deal with committee dysfunction.

Expression and Emotional Range Alpha’s default expression is neutral,
observant, and slightly weary.

Important recurring expressions include: - Neutral / observant. - Calm
authority. - Thoughtful. - Slight amusement / dry humor. - Concerned. -
Disapproving. - Irritated. - Disbelief. - Facepalm. - Genuine hearty
laughter. - Focused authority. - Anger, used rarely.

Alpha’s anger should be uncommon. When he becomes genuinely angry, it
should feel consequential.

His humor is generally dry and understated.

World Creation Only Alpha, as Council head, possesses the power to
create an entire world.

World creation is extraordinary even for him and consumes enormous
accumulated divine power.

When Alpha creates a world, supernatural effects may become vast and
spectacular.

This contrast is important: Alpha normally displays little or no visible
magical energy, making the scale of creation more impressive when he
actually exercises that power.

Shape Language Alpha’s visual identity is based on mass, simplicity, and
vertical weight rather than decorative motifs.

His silhouette should read as: - Very tall. - Broad shoulders. - Long
ivory robes. - Long white hair. - Enormous white beard. - Minimal
ornament.

He should resemble a monumental white column rather than an elaborately
decorated fantasy monarch.

Visual Differentiation Alpha is visually distinct from the other
principal divine characters through: - Male sex. - Apparent age
approximately 80. - Height approximately 6 feet 4 inches. - Broad
powerful build. - Pure-white shoulder-blade-length hair. - Extremely
long, full white beard. - Steel-blue eyes. - Heavy ivory/white robes. -
Minimal ornamentation. - No visible permanent magical aura.

Forbidden Visual Traits Do not render Alpha with: - A crown, tiara,
circlet, or royal headpiece. - Pointed or elven ears. - Short hair. -
Dark hair. - A short or narrow wizard beard. - A braided or jeweled
beard. - A frail, sickly, or decrepit body. - A youthful or merely
middle-aged face. - Excessive gold filigree. - Complicated belt
hardware. - Multiple dangling cords, chains, medallions, or tassels. - A
large emblem on the back of his robe. - A generic multi-point
star/compass symbol. - A permanent magical aura. - Floating runes or
magic circles when he is merely standing or speaking. - A stereotypical
wizard hat, staff, or wand unless explicitly required by the script. - A
throne as an intrinsic part of his character identity.

Production Reference Principle Alpha should remain immediately
identifiable from silhouette alone.

His strongest silhouette anchors are his exceptional height, broad
shoulders, long simple robes, shoulder-blade-length white hair, and
enormous upper-waist-length white beard.

From a head-and-shoulders view, the combination of clearly elderly
facial features, swept-back pure-white hair, massive white beard,
steel-blue eyes, and deeply experienced expression should make him
unmistakable.

Canonical character-sheet artwork and this textual specification should
be used together when generating or validating Alpha’s appearance.
