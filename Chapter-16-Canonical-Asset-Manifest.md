# Chapter 16 Canonical Asset Manifest

Manifest date: October 16, 2026

## Status definitions

- **Active:** current primary canonical reference.
- **Contextual:** canonical only for the stated costume, role, place, or scene.
- **Historical:** retained for an earlier age or timeline and must not override the current reference.
- **Supporting:** a canonical detail, prop, state, insignia, or specification used with a primary reference.
- **Redundant alias:** a byte-identical duplicate filename retained temporarily in the folder but not valid as a separate canonical reference.

## Step 20 classification

For the formal Step 20 classification, `Active`, `Contextual`, and `Historical` entries are **canonical** within their documented scope. `Supporting` entries are **supplemental**. The live folder contains 60 canonical assets and 9 supplemental assets.

Generated-on-the-fly material is not stored as a co-equal master. It is limited to anonymous background figures, incidental debris and atmosphere, generic room dressing, non-identified written material, and minor spell/mana/portal effects. Such material must inherit the bound canonical references and may not introduce a new named character, costume, insignia, vehicle, weapon, location design, reader-visible wording, or crystal/gauge state.

The complete machine-readable classification, Chapter 16 panel-reference counts, and retained-unbound list are recorded in `../CH16-Step20-Asset-Classification.json`.

Nyas and Zora are alternate names for the same world; `Zora/Nyas` and `Nyas/Zora` are intentional canonical forms. Nyssarians have pointed elven ears. English is permitted in production-sheet annotations. Nyssarian diegetic surfaces use Nyssarian script and base-12 symbols. Earth-built technology may use English. Exact Chapter 16 page and panel assignments are recorded in `Chapter-16-Script-to-Panel-Asset-Map.md`.
