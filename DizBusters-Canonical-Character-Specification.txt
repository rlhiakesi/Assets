SEVEN SCREAMING DIZ-BUSTERS
CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION
IAKESI / IMAGINOS PROJECT

STATUS
Canonical working specification. The approved character and costume sheets govern visual appearance; this file governs semantic canon, behavior, transformations, and continuity.

1. CORE CONCEPT

Exactly seven Diz-Busters exist:
DB-1 — The Organizer
DB-2 — The Muscle
DB-3 — The Performer
DB-4 — The Engineer
DB-5 — The Schemer
DB-6 — The Financier
DB-7 — The Explorer

All seven have the SAME natural anatomy, face, body proportions, height, skin color, hair, eyes, ears, hands, and feet.

They are seven independent persons, not clones and not a hive mind. Natural-form identity is communicated primarily by costume, personality, expression/body language, and DB number. Without costume/context, they cannot normally be distinguished physically.

2. NATURAL FORM

The natural form is overwhelmingly HUMANOID.

Canonical traits:
- Approximately 6'1" / 1.85 m.
- Lean, athletic humanoid build.
- Blue skin.
- Handsome/refined human-like face.
- Human-style hair, eyes, nose, mouth, and teeth.
- Two arms and two legs with ordinary humanoid proportions.
- Subtle webbed ears.
- Modest hand and foot webbing on land.
- No tail, scales, dorsal fin, facial fins, fish snout, bulbous fish eyes, claws, or visible external gills.
- No permanent large swimming fins.

From a distance the character should read as a blue humanoid, not a fish creature.

3. AQUATIC ADAPTATIONS

The Diz-Busters are aquatic/amphibious and cold-blooded.

WEBBED EARS
Humanoid ears with restrained fin-like webbing. The membranes sense water-pressure changes, current direction, low-frequency vibration, and nearby movement.

HANDS
Mostly human on land with modest finger webbing. Underwater, webbing expands for steering and maneuverability.

FEET
Mostly humanoid on land. Underwater, toe webbing expands and may extend slightly beyond the toes to form compact natural swim-fin surfaces.

FOREARM MEMBRANES
Narrow membranes recessed along the outer forearms. Retracted on land and nearly invisible except for subtle seams. They deploy reflexively underwater for steering.

CALF MEMBRANES
Narrow membranes recessed along the outer lower legs. Retracted on land; deployed underwater for thrust efficiency and stability.

SWIMMING
No jets. Propulsion comes from a highly flexible spine/hips, whole-body undulation, powerful legs, expanded foot webbing, and calf membranes. Hands and forearm membranes steer. At speed they move like streamlined human-shaped marine mammals rather than humans performing ordinary swim strokes.

RESPIRATION
They can remain underwater for extended periods. Aquatic respiration is internal/divine biology. Do not add external gills.

4. THERMOREGULATION

Diz-Busters are ectothermic/cold-blooded. They are adapted to Hell's extremely hot geothermal waters. Water comfortable to them may be dangerous to humans.

Cold Earth water, particularly the Potomac in winter, is uncomfortable and can impair them. Earth equipment therefore includes heated wet locks and recovery systems.

5. HUMAN TRANSFORMATION

Diz-Buster transformation is NOT open-ended shapeshifting.

Exactly three body configurations exist:
1. Natural Diz-Buster.
2. Human-M — one predefined male human.
3. Human-F — one predefined female human.

ALL SEVEN use exactly the SAME Human-M.
ALL SEVEN use exactly the SAME Human-F.

There are not seven variants of either human identity.

Human-M and Human-F have fixed face, body, height, hair, eye color, apparent age, and other physical traits.

A human transformation can last up to SIX DAYS. Six hours is NOT canonical.

Human transformation is energetically expensive and used purposefully. Human forms appear fully human: no blue skin, webbed anatomy, aquatic membranes, or other visible natural-form traits.

6. SHARED CONSCIOUSNESS

The Diz-Busters do NOT have a hive mind.

Each has an independent consciousness, personality, preferences, emotions, and judgment.

They can voluntarily share memories and operational experience when needed, including conversations, people, negotiations, promises, relationships, locations, passwords, and sensory memories.

This allows seamless substitution. One DB can conduct business as Human-M, share the relevant memories, revert, and another DB can assume Human-M and continue the same relationship with complete factual continuity.

Memory can be shared; personality is not. A perceptive human may notice subtle differences in manner between substitutions.

7. PERMANENT NATURAL-FORM IDENTITIES

Costume is the primary visual identifier and must not drift between DB numbers.

DB-1 — THE ORGANIZER
Personality: disciplined leader; keeps others focused; takes Lucy seriously.
Costume: black/navy; high structured/asymmetric collar; long divided lower panels; gold trim.

DB-2 — THE MUSCLE
Personality: direct, physical, favors force; often needs reminders about subtlety.
Costume: dark burgundy; diagonal chest strap; broad shoulder treatment; relatively exposed arms; reinforced construction.

DB-3 — THE PERFORMER
Personality: theatrical; loves audiences and human form; naturally conspicuous.
Costume: purple; theatrical/open front; long flowing divided panels; decorative gold accents.

DB-4 — THE ENGINEER
Personality: builder/inventor; maintains locks, boats, equipment, and field systems.
Costume: dark green; practical vest; utility belt; pockets/pouches; reinforced functional construction.

DB-5 — THE SCHEMER
Personality: secretive; plans within plans; best natural practitioner of Lucy's invisibility doctrine.
Costume: black/dark charcoal; hood/cowl; layered concealing folds; minimal ornamentation.

DB-6 — THE FINANCIER
Personality: sophisticated; Blue Oyster Cult liaison; understands investment, leverage, money, and institutional power.
Costume: dark navy; tailored formal cut; lapel-like collar; gold piping; most businesslike natural-form outfit.

DB-7 — THE EXPLORER
Personality: curious; fascinated by Earth, boats, travel, places, and human culture.
Costume: teal; asymmetric sash/wrap; flowing travel-ready lower panels; decorative patterning.

Permanent natural-form costumes NEVER transfer between DB numbers unless a script explicitly requires a deliberate costume exchange.

8. HUMAN-FORM WARDROBE

Natural-form costume identifies DB number. Human-form clothing does NOT.

Human-M and Human-F have mission-appropriate wardrobes belonging to the shared human identities. Clothing should not reveal which DB is currently operating the form unless explicitly scripted.

9. HELL

Hell is their true home after exile.

Surface: volcanic, extremely hot, black stone, ash, lava, violent geothermal activity.

Below the surface: geothermal seas, mineral pools, volcanic caverns, hydrothermal vents, and superheated water systems.

Their aquatic physiology is adapted to these geothermal waters.

Lucy remains physically confined to Hell. The seven Diz-Busters are permitted to travel between dimensions under Alpha's restrictions.

10. COLUMBIA ISLAND MARINA

Columbia Island Marina is the terminal Washington node and the Diz-Busters' principal Earth field station.

Seven ordinary-looking, deliberately dissimilar boats are associated with the seven DBs. They must not look like an obvious supernatural fleet.

Each contains a concealed heated wet lock / SCUBA port below the waterline.

Typical arrival:
1. DB approaches underwater in natural form.
2. Hidden hull access opens.
3. DB enters wet chamber.
4. Hatch seals.
5. Water is heated if necessary.
6. DB enters the boat unseen.
7. If needed, DB assumes Human-M or Human-F inside.
8. Human identity exits conventionally onto the dock.

Departure reverses the sequence.

The boats are access points, changing facilities, equipment/communications stations, node-maintenance stations, and temporary shelters. They are NOT permanent residences. Hell remains headquarters.

11. BLUE OYSTER CULT RELATIONSHIP

The real Blue Oyster Cult is Lucy's terrestrial human implementation organization.

The Diz-Busters supervise and interact with it as needed. DB-6 is the primary financial/institutional liaison, but any DB can use the shared human identities and substitute for another through shared memory.

The Cult supplies money, investments, political influence, legal structures, property, transportation, documents, human labor, and institutional access.

The Diz-Busters should not personally accumulate conspicuous terrestrial fortunes.

12. RELATIONSHIP TO IMAGINOS

Imaginos has much broader shapeshifting than the Diz-Busters.

Diz-Busters:
Natural <-> Human-M <-> Human-F only.

Imaginos:
Can construct substantially different human identities, subject to high mana cost.

Do not conflate Diz-Buster shared consciousness with Imaginos's temporal duplication.

The Blue Oyster Cult performs most routine historical manipulation.
The Diz-Busters supervise.
Imaginos is the special-operations asset used when ordinary influence fails or supernatural intervention is required.

13. RENDERING / CONTINUITY RULES

MANDATORY:
- Exactly seven Diz-Busters exist.
- Natural anatomy is identical across all seven.
- Same face, hairstyle, height, build, blue skin tone, ear anatomy, and hand/foot anatomy.
- Distinguish by costume and behavior, not anatomy.
- DB number means identity/personality/costume, not body type.
- Swimming membranes remain retracted on land unless explicitly deployed.
- Deployed membranes are functional and restrained, never huge decorative fins.
- Human-M is identical regardless of which DB operates it.
- Human-F is identical regardless of which DB operates it.
- Human clothing does not identify the underlying DB by default.

14. EXPLICIT PROHIBITIONS

DO NOT:
- Make the seven different-looking blue humanoids.
- Give them unique faces, hairstyles, heights, musculature, skin tones, or ear shapes.
- Add scales, fish heads/mouths, snouts, bulbous fish eyes, external gills, tails, dorsal fins, or claws.
- Render them as Creature-from-the-Black-Lagoon-style monsters.
- Use biological or technological water jets.
- Give Diz-Busters arbitrary/open-ended shapeshifting.
- Create seven different Human-M or Human-F identities.
- Reduce transformation duration to six hours; the maximum is SIX DAYS.
- Treat shared consciousness as a hive mind.
- Transfer permanent DB costumes without explicit story instruction.
- Treat the marina boats as their permanent home.
- Forget that they are cold-blooded and adapted to Hell's geothermal water.
