COUNCIL OF THE GODS (COG) TABLE & CHAIRS — CANONICAL PRODUCTION
SPECIFICATION

PURPOSE Defines the canonical COG table, chairs, seating geometry,
standing positions, and immediate Council Chamber environment. These are
persistent props and world-space constraints.

TABLE Shape: Circular. Surface: Completely plain polished deep
warm-brown wood. Approximate diameter: 9 meters. Edge: Simple restrained
molding with optional narrow warm-gold accent. Base: Sturdy dark wood,
visually simple.

The tabletop has NO carvings, inlays, runes, writing, magic circles,
compass roses, starbursts, central emblems, or symbolic patterns.
Natural wood grain is material texture only.

CHAIRS EXACTLY EIGHT CHAIRS.

Never render seven or nine chairs. Never add a chair for Terra, Zorna,
or Nyssara.

All eight use the same canonical chair family: - High back. - Deep-blue
upholstered back and seat. - Warm-gold frame. - Armrests with restrained
curved ends. - Four stable carved legs. - Ornate but repeatable upper
crest. - No generic multi-point star/compass motif.

Alpha’s 12:00 chair belongs to the same chair family; it is not a
separate throne.

Approximate chair dimensions: Overall height: 1.55 m. Width: 0.65–0.80
m. Depth: 0.70–0.80 m. Seat height: 0.50 m.

CANONICAL WORLD-SPACE POSITIONS Clock positions are fixed in world space
and do not change with camera angle.

12:00 — Alpha 10:00 — Aurelia 9:00 — Pyrael 7:30 — Verdania 6:00 —
Mortheus 4:30 — Lunaria 3:00 — Thalassor 1:30 — Vortex

There is exactly one chair at each of these positions.

IMPORTANT: The chairs are NOT evenly spaced at 45-degree intervals. Do
not distribute them automatically around the circle.

STANDING POSITIONS 11:00 — Intentionally open standing area for Zorna
and Nyssara, or other visitors appearing before the Council. It must
accommodate Zorna and Nyssara together. No permanent chair.

1:00 — Intentionally open standing area for Terra, between Alpha at
12:00 and Vortex at 1:30. Terra has no Council chair. No permanent
chair.

BLOCKING RULE Council positions are world-space constraints.
Screen-left/screen-right ordering may change with the camera, but
world-space positions do not.

COUNCIL CHAMBER ENVIRONMENT Architecture: - Monumental pale stone/marble
columns. - Grand, open divine architecture. - Luminous clouds may be
visible beyond the columns. - Environment should feel open and immense.

Floor: - Polished pale stone/marble. - Subtle reflections are
acceptable. - No symbolic floor patterns, magic circles, compass
designs, starbursts, runes, or emblems.

LIGHTING Lighting comes from the ambient environment of the Gods.

No candelabras, candles, torches, lamps, chandeliers, lanterns,
electrical fixtures, or visible magical lamps.

The sun exists above the visible ceiling/roofline and is never in frame.

Avoid direct solar glare, lens flare, blown highlights, harsh
backlighting, or silhouettes.

Lighting should be bright, warm, diffuse, soft, even, apparently
sourceless, and suitable for CMYK printing. Faces, costumes, table
details, and chair details must remain readable.

MATERIALS AND COLORS Table: Deep polished warm-brown wood. Table accent:
Restrained warm gold. Chair frames: Polished warm/divine gold. Chair
upholstery: Deep blue. Floor/columns: Pale ivory, cream, or warm-white
stone/marble. Lighting: Warm diffuse ambient divine illumination.

CANONICAL CONTINUITY RULES 1. Circular table. 2. Plain tabletop with no
symbols or intentional decorative pattern. 3. Exactly eight chairs. 4.
Chairs occupy the eight individually specified clock positions. 5. Chair
spacing is not uniformly 45 degrees. 6. 11:00 remains open for standing
visitors. 7. 1:00 remains open for Terra. 8. Terra has no permanent
Council chair. 9. All eight chairs use the same canonical design family.
10. No additional chairs unless explicitly required by script. 11. Pale
monumental columns and polished pale floor. 12. Clouds may be visible
beyond the open architecture. 13. Lighting is ambient
divine/environmental light. 14. No conventional lighting fixtures. 15.
Sun never visible in the Council Chamber. 16. No generic multi-point
star/compass motif on table, floor, chairs, or architecture.

FORBIDDEN ELEMENTS - Seven-chair or nine-chair layouts. - Chair at
11:00. - Chair at 1:00. - Chair for Terra. - Chair for standing
visitors. - Uniform 45-degree chair spacing. - Compass roses. -
Multi-point starbursts. - Magic circles. - Runes or writing on
table/floor. - Central table emblems. - Symbolic tabletop inlays. -
Decorative floor symbols. - Candelabras, candles, torches, lamps, or
chandeliers. - Visible sun or lens flare. - Harsh direct sunlight. -
Random tabletop props. - Carpets/rugs or extra furniture unless
explicitly scripted.

SBL PRODUCTION NOTE Represent the Council layout as explicit world-space
blocking. The renderer must NOT calculate chair locations by dividing
360 degrees by eight.

Chair positions: Alpha = 12:00 Aurelia = 10:00 Pyrael = 9:00 Verdania =
7:30 Mortheus = 6:00 Lunaria = 4:30 Thalassor = 3:00 Vortex = 1:30

Standing positions: Zorna/Nyssara visitor position = approximately 11:00
Terra = approximately 1:00

These semantic world-space constraints remain invariant under camera
movement.

AUTHORITY RULE Use the canonical production-sheet artwork and this
textual specification together. If production-sheet artwork accidentally
depicts an incorrect number of chairs or conflicts with the explicit
seating geometry, THIS TEXTUAL SPECIFICATION IS AUTHORITATIVE FOR CHAIR
COUNT AND POSITION.

There are exactly eight Council chairs.
