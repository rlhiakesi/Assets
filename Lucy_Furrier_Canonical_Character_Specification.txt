LUCY FURRIER — CANONICAL CHARACTER SPECIFICATION

Identity
Lucy Furrier is a female Second-Tier Goddess in the Dimension of the Gods. She is extraordinarily beautiful, powerful, capable, ambitious, and fully aware of all of these qualities. In Chapter 19 she seeks promotion to First-Tier status and becomes the central figure in the Acolyte rebellion after the Council rejects her advancement.

Apparent Age
Approximately 32–35. This is a visual age target only. Lucy must read as a fully mature adult woman.

Height
Approximately 5 feet 11 inches (180 cm).

Build
Tall, slender, statuesque, and graceful. Lucy has a naturally feminine adult figure with balanced proportions. She is neither petite nor heavily muscular.

Her physical design must not rely on exaggerated breast size, an unnaturally narrow waist, extreme hip proportions, or other sexualized anatomical exaggeration. Her beauty comes primarily from her face, posture, hair, controlled movement, and commanding presence.

Face
Lucy is intended to be stunningly beautiful in a sophisticated, self-aware, aristocratic manner.

Her face is refined and highly symmetrical, with an elegant oval-to-subtle-heart shape, high but natural cheekbones, a clean jawline, refined chin, straight proportionate nose, and naturally full but not exaggerated lips.

Her facial construction must remain clearly adult. Avoid generic cute-anime-girl proportions, oversized childlike eyes, adolescent facial proportions, or exaggerated glamour-model anatomy.

Lucy knows that she is beautiful. Her default expression should communicate calm confidence rather than overt seduction.

Eyes
Amber-gold to molten golden-orange. The eyes are one of Lucy’s major identifying features and must remain consistent.

They are refined almond-shaped adult eyes with detailed irises, controlled lashes, and a direct, assessing gaze. In dramatic lighting the irises may appear warmer or more orange, but they should not become red.

Skin
Fair to lightly warm fair. Natural healthy adult complexion.

Ears
Normal human-shaped ears. Neither pointed nor elongated.

Hair
Lucy’s hair is extremely long, predominantly straight, smooth, and controlled. It extends approximately to the upper thighs.

The hair has a distinctive red tonal progression:
- Roots: very deep burgundy/crimson.
- Mid-length: saturated crimson.
- Ends: hot scarlet/red-copper.

The transition should appear as believable hair coloration rather than magical glowing fire.

The bulk of the hair is swept backward away from the forehead. Lucy has no bangs.

Two long, controlled face-framing sections may fall forward on either side of the face. These should remain subordinate to the large, smooth rear hair mass.

The lower hair silhouette may separate into several long tapered sections, but it must not resemble Zorna’s drill curls, Terra’s short voluminous waves, Pyrael’s high-volume tied-back red hair, Lunaria’s lavender-silver asymmetrical hair, or Nivara’s black waves.

Do not add braids, horns, tiaras, crowns, or elaborate hair ornaments.

Beauty Direction
Lucy is the “self-aware magnificent beauty” of the divine cast.

She should be exceptionally beautiful without relying on exaggerated anatomy, excessive exposed skin, conspicuous breast emphasis, or provocative posing.

Her appeal comes from exceptional facial beauty, amber-gold eyes, extremely long controlled crimson hair, statuesque proportions, immaculate posture, elegant movement, sophisticated costume design, and absolute confidence.

Lucy should look like someone accustomed to being the most impressive person in a room and genuinely surprised when others fail to recognize her superiority.

Her beauty is deliberate and polished, unlike Nyssara’s innocent natural beauty. It is more controlled and aristocratic than Zorna’s glamorous sophistication and less physically aggressive than Pyrael’s powerful beauty.

Ceremonial / Default Divine Outfit
Lucy’s principal Chapter 19 costume is an elegant ceremonial divine gown built around ivory, hot scarlet, and restrained warm gold.

The costume must remain visually distinct from Pyrael’s red-and-gold warrior-like attire.

The central body of Lucy’s gown is predominantly ivory or warm white. Scarlet structural elements frame the ivory center from the shoulders through the torso and continue into long outer skirt panels.

Gold trim is permitted but should remain controlled and architectural rather than densely ornamental.

The costume should emphasize vertical lines, height, precision, and authority.

The neckline may be elegant and feminine but should not rely on deep cleavage. Lucy should remain visually striking even when the costume is comparatively covered.

High Split Collar
Lucy’s ceremonial gown has a distinctive high split collar rising behind and beside the neck. The collar creates two narrow outward/upward points as part of the garment construction.

These are fabric/costume structures only. They must not resemble biological horns, demonic horns, or a horned crown.

The collar is an important upper-body silhouette feature and should remain recognizable in medium and long shots.

Trailing Sash / Costume “Tail”
Lucy’s signature costume feature is a single long scarlet trailing sash extending from the rear/lower portion of the ceremonial gown. It is clothing, not anatomy.

The sash is asymmetrical: there is only one principal trailing element rather than two conventional coat tails.

It is long enough to trail visibly behind Lucy and tends to form a graceful broad S-curve when she walks or stands dramatically. The fabric has enough body to preserve a recognizable curved silhouette while still behaving as fabric.

The terminal end has only the faintest suggestion of a point. It should primarily read as a tapered decorative fabric end. The devil-tail association must be subtle enough that it may not be consciously noticed before Lucy’s exile to Hell.

Do not use a large arrowhead, spearhead, heart-shaped devil-tail tip, rigid weapon-like point, organic tail, or prehensile tail. The point should be barely present.

The sash is not alive and does not move independently. Its apparent expressiveness comes only from gravity, fabric momentum, wind, Lucy’s movement, and artistic composition.

The trailing sash is a major silhouette identifier and should remain visible whenever framing reasonably permits.

Footwear
Elegant gold or ivory-and-gold heeled footwear appropriate to the ceremonial gown. Refined rather than armored.

Jewelry
Jewelry should be minimal and restrained. Lucy’s visual identity should not depend on necklaces, crowns, tiaras, elaborate earrings, or gemstone-heavy ornamentation.

Posture and Movement
Lucy stands exceptionally straight and carries herself with deliberate elegance. She rarely slouches, fidgets, or adopts uncertain body language.

Her gestures are controlled and economical. She tends to occupy space as though she assumes others will accommodate her.

When listening, Lucy often appears to evaluate the speaker rather than passively receive information.

When walking, her long hair and trailing scarlet sash create controlled secondary motion behind an otherwise composed figure.

Personality Expressed Visually
Brilliant, ambitious, charismatic, capable, persuasive, proud, calculating, self-assured, commanding, impatient with perceived inferiority, and increasingly contemptuous when denied recognition.

Lucy should not default to overt villain expressions. Before her rejection by the Council, her confidence is sincere and largely effortless. She genuinely expects promotion.

Her early smiles can be charming and persuasive. Her condescension should often be subtle: a slight eyebrow movement, restrained smile, measured gaze, or momentary disbelief.

The Council’s rejection should visibly crack her normally immaculate composure.

Expression Set
Important canonical expression targets include: confident / neutral; charming smile; amused; condescending; analytical / evaluating; irritated; incredulous; humiliated; controlled anger; furious; calculating; persuasive; triumphant.

Her furious expression should feel especially powerful because uncontrolled emotion is unusual for her.

Visual Differentiation from Pyrael
Both Lucy and Pyrael use red prominently, so they must remain immediately distinguishable.

Pyrael is tall, muscular, powerfully athletic, with deep-red high-volume hair, warm bronze skin, and red-and-gold warrior-like ceremonial attire. Her visual language is flame, passion, physical strength, and direct action.

Lucy is tall, slender, statuesque, fair-skinned, with extremely long predominantly straight crimson hair, an ivory-centered gown framed by hotter scarlet, a high split garment collar, and a single long curved trailing sash. Her visual language is elegance, perfection, ambition, authority, and control.

In silhouette, Lucy and Pyrael must never be mistaken for one another.

Visual Differentiation from Aurelia
Aurelia has short pale-blonde hair and a clean white-and-gold geometric silhouette. Lucy has extremely long crimson hair, strong scarlet outer panels, a high split collar, and a long asymmetric trailing sash. Do not allow Lucy’s ivory central gown to make her read as a red-haired variation of Aurelia.

Visual Differentiation from Lunaria
Lunaria is very tall and willowy, with lavender-silver hair and a flowing lavender formal gown. Lucy is statuesque rather than willowy, with extremely long crimson hair, stronger architectural costume construction, hot scarlet outer panels, and the signature trailing sash.

Visual Differentiation from Nivara
Nivara has midnight-black wavy hair, deep-violet clothing, conspicuous purple gemstones, and an ornate sacred-law aesthetic. Lucy has predominantly straight crimson hair, amber-gold eyes, ivory/scarlet clothing, restrained jewelry, and no forehead jewel or tiara.

Visual Differentiation from Terra
Terra appears approximately 45, has a mature curvaceous build, moss-green eyes, thick auburn/copper hair slightly shorter than shoulder length, a strong side part, large loose waves, and a living-leaf ornament. Lucy is younger in apparent age, taller, more slender, has dramatically longer and straighter crimson hair, amber-gold eyes, and a sharply controlled scarlet/ivory silhouette.

Visual Differentiation from Verdania
Verdania appears approximately 64 and has a strong, full-figured nurturing build, gray-brown hair arranged in a mature bun, living blossoms, and green/ivory life-themed attire. Lucy must read as a substantially younger, taller, more angular and aristocratic figure with long crimson hair and no floral ornamentation.

Visual Differentiation from Zorna
Zorna is tall, graceful, platinum blonde, and defined by extremely long abundant drill/corkscrew curls and white/deep-blue formal styling. Lucy’s hair is crimson, predominantly straight, and controlled rather than curled. Her costume is ivory/hot-scarlet/gold and her single curved trailing sash creates a lower silhouette unlike Zorna’s layered gown.

Visual Differentiation from Nyssara
Nyssara is petite, apparent age approximately 30, with straight black shoulder-blade-length hair, bangs, violet eyes, visible elven ears, and a contained observational presence. Lucy is much taller, has human ears, no bangs, extremely long crimson hair, amber-gold eyes, and an expansive commanding presence.

Nyssara’s beauty is innocent and largely unselfconscious. Lucy’s beauty is sophisticated, deliberate, and fully understood by Lucy herself.

Hell Foreshadowing
Lucy’s original DOTG design should contain subtle visual elements that acquire additional meaning after her exile to Hell: hot scarlet coloration; high split pointed collar; amber-gold eyes; long scarlet trailing sash with a barely perceptible tapered point; and a strong pale-center/red-frame costume structure.

Before exile, these elements must read as elegant divine fashion.

Lucy must not possess biological horns, an organic tail, demonic wings, cloven hooves, a pitchfork, or other explicit devil anatomy in the Dimension of the Gods.

The visual joke should become clearer only after the same design is placed against Hell’s volcanic environment.

Forbidden Visual Traits
Do not render Lucy with biological horns; an organic or prehensile tail; a large arrowhead or heart-shaped tip on the costume sash; wings; cloven hooves; a pitchfork; demonic anatomy in the Dimension of the Gods; pointed/elven ears; a crown or tiara; bangs; Zorna-like drill curls; Terra-like short voluminous curls; Pyrael’s tied-back/high-volume warrior hairstyle; black hair resembling Nivara or Nyssara; lavender hair resembling Lunaria; exaggerated breast size; adolescent proportions; extreme cleavage; overtly sexual posing as her default presentation; heavy gemstone jewelry; generic flame armor; Pyrael’s warrior silhouette; or a multi-point star or compass motif.

Color Palette
Hair roots: deep burgundy/crimson.
Hair mid-length: saturated crimson.
Hair ends: hot scarlet/red-copper.
Eyes: amber-gold / molten golden-orange.
Skin: fair to lightly warm fair.
Primary outer fabric: hot scarlet.
Central/secondary fabric: ivory or warm white.
Trim/metal: restrained warm gold.

Colors must remain sufficiently separated in value and saturation to preserve costume construction and hair detail after CMYK conversion.

Silhouette Requirement
Lucy must remain identifiable from silhouette alone.

Primary silhouette identifiers are a tall statuesque adult figure, extremely long predominantly straight hair, high split garment collar, long vertical ceremonial gown, and single asymmetric trailing sash forming a broad S-curve.

The trailing sash should provide a distinctive lower-body silhouette without becoming an obvious devil tail.

From a head-and-shoulders view, the combination of refined adult face, no bangs, swept-back crimson hair, amber-gold eyes, human ears, and high split collar should make Lucy recognizable.

From side and rear views, the extremely long tapered hair mass and single trailing sash should distinguish her from every other principal Goddess.

Production Reference Principle
Lucy’s canonical character-sheet artwork and this textual specification should be used together when generating or validating her appearance.

The character sheet defines the approved visual design. This specification clarifies the features that must remain stable across different poses, camera angles, rendering styles, lighting conditions, and future chapters.

When Lucy appears with other Gods and Goddesses, preserve her individual identity rather than averaging her design with nearby red-, white-, or gold-themed characters.

Her core visual read is: stunningly beautiful + statuesque + extremely long controlled crimson hair + amber-gold eyes + ivory/hot-scarlet/gold ceremonial attire + high split collar + subtle single trailing sash + absolute self-confidence.
